# Public Reading and Release Statement

**Date:** 22 September 2026  
**Project:** Geonosis / Reading the Ancient Archive  
**Author:** Sean Diamond

> **At present, this reading is being carried by one active reader. The purpose of publication is to make sure it does not remain that way.**

This is a statement about the present state of the work, not a claim of exclusive ability.

The project position is specific: Sean Diamond is presently carrying the sustained, corpus-wide Geonosis / Méthodê Glyph reading programme. That does **not** mean other people cannot read the archive. The method is designed so that they can.

The desired outcome is redundancy.

A successful public research programme should make the original reader progressively less necessary by exposing enough source material, derivation, controls, corrections and testable predictions that another person can reproduce, challenge, extend or replace the reading without needing privileged access to the researcher.

## Release ethic

Geonosis therefore follows these principles:

1. **Open by default.** Research should be publicly inspectable wherever rights, privacy, heritage sensitivity and safety allow.
2. **No paywall as epistemic gate.** Public understanding should not depend on purchasing access to the central derivation.
3. **Show the staircase.** Release the source, the visible relation, the derivation, the alternatives, the prediction and the failure condition where available.
4. **Make correction public.** Earlier versions remain part of the record; later corrections do not silently erase them.
5. **Invite independent readers.** Agreement is not required. Reproduction, counter-evidence and stronger derivation are welcome.
6. **Separate openness from recklessness.** If a future result becomes experimentally reproducible and operationally hazardous, the public evidence and finding can remain open while dangerous implementation parameters are handled under an appropriate safety boundary.
7. **Do not confuse single-reader status with ownership.** The archive is not a private possession because one person is currently doing the reading.

## Why publish

If a reading remains in one mind, it is fragile.

If it is documented, cited, versioned, tested and made public, it can become independent of the person who first assembled it.

That is the point.

> **The goal is not to remain indispensable. The goal is to make the work reproducible enough that the researcher can become redundant.**
