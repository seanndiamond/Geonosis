# Geonosis Public Constitution

This file is the public, research-facing extract of the current Geonosis constitutional method. It does not replace the canonical archive source. It exposes the rules that govern public claims.

## 1. Field Before Form

**Field precedes form. Form remembers field.**

Begin with relation, position, boundary, direction, scale, sequence, pressure, environment and context before allowing a familiar category to close the reading.

## 2. Method Before Claim

The public front door is the method. Claims enter only after the visible pathway is shown.

State the research question before the conclusion. Show working, alternatives, controls and failure conditions.

## 3. Image Before Inherited Explanation

Look first. Name second. Read before reducing.

Visible posture, placement, direction, relation, scale, sequence and recurrence are inspected before inherited labels are allowed to govern interpretation.

## 4. A Caption Is Not a Translation

A caption is not a translation. A classification is not an audit. A label is not a reading.

Institutional labels may orient inquiry. They do not remove the burden of derivation.

## 5. Constraint First

**If nothing constrains, nothing proceeds.**

A valid reading must be limited by boundary, adjacency, direction, register, slot, sequence, scale behaviour, operator placement or another testable structural condition.

## 6. No Isolated System Reading

A single token, shape or resemblance cannot demonstrate a system without adjacency, recurrence, sequence or relational grammar.

Isolated forms may be recorded as candidate motifs. They do not establish executable meaning.

## 7. Visible Derivation

A scientific reading must expose the chain from visible artefact to final interpretation.

Where relevant, show:

1. original artefact or source field;
2. exact sign or feature location;
3. sign/feature recognition;
4. materially plausible values or interpretations;
5. reason one value is selected;
6. transliteration or technical classification where applicable;
7. grammatical, operational, spatial or construction step;
8. final interpretation;
9. behavioural or comparative control.

If the chain cannot be shown, report the claim as inherited expert reconstruction or unresolved interpretation rather than visibly demonstrated proof.

## 8. Equal Evidentiary Rule

The same class of evidence must testify under the same rule whether the conclusion is conventional or Geonosis.

Conventional names receive no immunity from visual audit. Geonosis receives no lower burden because it is unconventional.

## 9. Observation and Interpretation Are Separate Rooms

Describe what is visible before stating what it means.

When needed, label statements as:

- direct observation;
- inherited designation;
- operator interpretation;
- grammar hypothesis;
- speculative reconstruction;
- external validation.

The rooms may communicate. They may not be quietly collapsed.

## 10. Return Test

A reading gains force when it returns coherent, practical, transferable or predictive understanding.

Return does not replace visual evidence. A beneficial personal result does not prove a historical reconstruction.

## 11. Prediction Must Precede Inspection

For predictive testing, log the expected feature, relation or outcome before inspecting the target evidence.

Failed predictions remain in the archive.

## 12. Failure Must Be Possible

A framework that cannot lose is not a research framework.

Every consequential claim should identify what would make it weaken, fail, narrow in scope or return inconclusive.

## 13. Corrections Are Additive

The project may learn without pretending the earlier path never existed.

A correction must:

- identify the earlier formulation;
- state the new formulation;
- explain why it changed;
- identify affected records;
- preserve source lineage;
- retain the earlier source.

Silent overwriting and retroactive certainty are prohibited.

## 14. Tools Test; They Do Not Decide

AI, GlyphLens, Cyclops, Gigantor, overlays, grids, rotations, image processing and other tools may assist inspection, comparison and audit.

They are not authorised to manufacture historical certainty, invent missing operators, conceal contradictory evidence, or substitute for source inspection.

## 15. Cultural and Ethical Floor

- Do not overwrite a living culture with an unconstrained story.
- Preserve provenance, local names, custody and image rights.
- Do not turn communities into scenery for a theory.
- Separate criticism of a reading from contempt for people.
- Withhold sensitive coordinates where disclosure creates heritage risk.
- Larger civilisational claims carry a larger burden of derivation and care.

## 16. Institutional Audit Law

Authority identifies a claim. It does not complete its derivation.

Consensus demonstrates social uptake, not automatic artefact-level correctness. A conventional reading may be correct even where its public derivation remains incomplete. Failure to locate that derivation must be reported honestly.

Preferred questions:

- Where is the derivation?
- What is visible before the label is applied?
- Why is this value selected over alternatives?
- Does the same rule work in a control example?
- What would falsify this reading?
- Which evidence room is this statement in?

## 17. Law of Prompted Advocacy

A generative model asked to establish, defend, refute, explain, justify or optimise a conclusion is acting as an advocate for that prompt.

Its output does not constitute evidence for the conclusion merely because it is fluent, detailed, cited, confident, internally coherent, or repeatedly generated.

**The prompt is part of the provenance of the resulting claim.**

A conclusion-first prompt must be marked as `ADVOCATE_MODE`. Advocate-mode output cannot count as independent confirmation of the desired conclusion.

**An AI can argue both sides. Therefore the argument cannot adjudicate itself.**

## 18. Prompt Provenance Law

Every consequential AI-assisted prosecution should preserve a prompt provenance envelope containing, at minimum:

- the exact initiating prompt;
- the assigned model role;
- whether a desired conclusion was supplied;
- the primary evidence supplied;
- the state of inherited institutional semantics;
- claims generated;
- highest Court stage actually earned;
- missing bridges;
- tripwires triggered;
- counter-role result where used;
- corrections;
- final Court state.

Prompt history may explain how a claim was generated. It does not prove the claim.

## 19. Adversarial Provenance Cycle

Consequential cases should, where practical, pass through four distinct operations:

1. **Blind Evidence Pass** — inspect the primary field without allowing inherited semantics to seed the observation.
2. **Strongest Institutional Case** — release the inherited interpretation as a source claim and construct its strongest explicit derivation graph.
3. **Adversarial Attack** — attack the derivation itself, testing independence, alternatives, circularity, selection rules, source substitution and stage promotion.
4. **Court Reconciliation** — assign only the highest evidentiary state earned by the graph.

The Court is not required to choose a winner. `UNKNOWN`, `NOT_DERIVED`, `INCONCLUSIVE` and `CONTESTED` are valid successful outcomes when they accurately mark the frontier.

## 20. Evidence Has the Final Chair

No model, institution, prosecutor, prior verdict, memory, sign list, catalogue, translation, citation network or research programme may promote a claim beyond the evidence actually shown.

**The model does not get the final chair. The evidence does.**

When a derivation terminates, the termination is itself a finding until new evidence reopens the case.

## Closing rule

**Geonosis must accept the same scrutiny it requests.**
