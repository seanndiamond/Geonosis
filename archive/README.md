# Geonosis Reproducible Public Archive

This directory is the audit spine of the public Geonosis repository.

The purpose is not merely to preserve conclusions. It is to preserve **how a conclusion came to exist**, what evidence room it occupies, what would defeat it, what changed over time, and enough provenance for another observer to repeat the inspection.

## Archive law

The repository follows this authority order:

1. **Raw source field and provenance** — artefact, terrain, image, body, sequence, observation, source document.
2. **Preservation and correction lineage** — original source remains; corrections are additive and dated.
3. **Foundational method law** — project-wide rules and definitions.
4. **Case-specific evidence records** — observations, controls, alternatives, claim status.
5. **Public outputs** — papers, books, videos, apps, summaries.
6. **Handoffs and compact summaries** — navigation aids, never independent proof.

## What reproducible means here

A Geonosis evidence package should allow an independent observer to answer:

- What exactly is the source?
- Where can I inspect it?
- What part of it is being claimed?
- What was visible before interpretation?
- What transformations, rotations, crops, overlays, measurements, or AI tools were used?
- What alternatives were considered?
- What constraint made one reading preferable?
- What would make the reading fail?
- Can I repeat the observation without being coached toward the answer?
- Has the claim changed, and if so, why?

## Non-erasure rule

The archive does not make old mistakes disappear for neatness.

When a reading changes:

1. preserve the earlier record;
2. state the new formulation;
3. state why it changed;
4. identify the evidence that caused the change;
5. link affected cases and outputs;
6. update confidence without pretending the new state was always known.

A research archive that remembers only its victories is advertising. This one is intended to remember the bruises as well.
