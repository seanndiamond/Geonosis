## Source proposition

A user-supplied YouTube transcript describes an engineering expert who allegedly prompted ChatGPT to “show how 3M is 0% at fault” in litigation concerning the Watson Grinding explosion, then later asked the model to review the resulting report as opposing counsel.

## Why the Court retains it

The case is not admitted as proof of the underlying legal facts without independent source prosecution. It is retained as an external illustration of a hazard already observed independently in Court OGI performance:

1. a conclusion-first prompt can place a generative model into advocacy mode;
2. the model can generate a persuasive argument toward the supplied destination;
3. a role reversal can cause the same model to attack the resulting argument;
4. therefore model fluency, confidence, citations, self-rating, and role-conditioned advocacy cannot adjudicate truth.
