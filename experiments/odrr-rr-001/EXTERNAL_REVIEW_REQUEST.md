# External Methods Review Request — ODRR-RR-001

## Reviewer brief

We are seeking adversarial pre-data review of a proposed blinded visual-perception replication. The originating investigators developed the pilot observation, so the purpose of this review is **not endorsement**. It is to identify defects before confirmatory participants are run.

### Narrow claim under test

Whether pre-identified pilot image families produce a larger preregistered ODRR score than matched controls under blinded randomized presentation.

This protocol does not test intentional historical encoding or the wider Geonosis framework.

## Please challenge

1. Is the research question narrow enough to answer empirically?
2. Does the ODRR composite measure a coherent construct, or does it combine incompatible judgments?
3. Are `coherence`, `grouping_change`, and `mere_rotation` understandable and separable to naive observers?
4. Are QC-ROT-STABLE and QC-REARRANGED valid outcome-neutral checks?
5. Is ±0.75 ODRR points defensible as a smallest effect of interest?
6. Is N=60 / dz=.40 planning adequate for the proposed inference?
7. Is the participant the correct primary unit?
8. Is the paired sign-flip test appropriate?
9. Are target/control image-matching variables sufficient?
10. Does the design adequately handle familiarity, iconic-source recognition, priming and enhancement?
11. Could any researcher decision after data collection still change which outcome class is reached?
12. What result would *you* regard as falsifying or materially downgrading the claim?

## Requested reviewer output

For each issue, mark:
- `ACCEPTABLE`;
- `REVISE BEFORE DATA`;
- `FATAL TO CURRENT DESIGN`;
- `OPTIONAL IMPROVEMENT`.

Please state the reason and, where possible, a concrete repair.

Reviews, including hostile reviews, may be archived publicly with permission. Protocol changes will preserve correction lineage rather than overwrite earlier versions.
