# Ethics and Research Governance — ODRR-RR-001

## Current gate

**No confirmatory human-participant collection is authorized by this repository document alone.**

This study involves adult human observers providing perceptual ratings. Before recruitment begins, the research must follow an appropriate ethics/research-governance review pathway and the resulting approval, exemption or other formal determination must be documented.

## Risk-minimization design

The protocol is intentionally designed to be low burden:
- adult participants only;
- ordinary visual stimuli;
- no deception about risk;
- no collection of names unless separately required by an approved consent process;
- no health, political, religious, sexual, criminal or similarly sensitive data;
- no precise geolocation;
- no unnecessary demographic profiling;
- pseudonymous participant tokens;
- withdrawal instructions included in the participant information sheet;
- anonymized public release only where permitted.

## Data minimization

Confirmatory dataset should contain only:
- participant token;
- session timestamp or coarse date as approved;
- trial/stimulus ID;
- orientation;
- ratings;
- task-check results;
- source-recognition response;
- broad prior-familiarity category;
- technical status.

## Required documents before recruitment

- ethics/review determination;
- participant information statement;
- consent form/process;
- data-management plan;
- withdrawal process;
- approved recruitment wording;
- retention/destruction plan;
- public-data release statement.

## Conflict and independence statement

The originating researcher and AI collaborator developed the pilot hypothesis. That fact is disclosed. Independent observers are used specifically because investigator expectancy is a known limitation.

## Publication boundary

No GitHub README, paper, preprint or public statement may describe confirmatory human replication as completed until:
- valid participants were actually run;
- exclusions were applied before unblinding where possible;
- blind key was verified and revealed;
- preregistered analysis was executed;
- the result classification was archived.
