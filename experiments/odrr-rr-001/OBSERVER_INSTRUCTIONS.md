# Observer Instructions — ODRR-RR-001

## Participant-facing text

You will view a series of images shown in several orientations.

The task is about **visual organization under changes of orientation**. There are no right or wrong historical interpretations, and you do not need specialist knowledge.

For each view, rate what you experience from the image in front of you.

### Rating A — Coherence
**How coherent or organized does this view appear?**

0 = no coherent organization  
10 = very strong coherent organization

### Rating B — Grouping change
For views after the first presentation of a source:

**Compared with the previous view of this same source, how much did your dominant grouping or interpretation change?**

0 = no meaningful change  
10 = very strong change

### Rating C — Mere rotation
**How much does this feel like the same organization simply turned?**

0 = not merely the same organization turned  
10 = entirely the same organization turned

### Optional neutral description
In one short sentence, describe the dominant organization you noticed. Avoid guessing the date, culture, identity, language or intended meaning unless you genuinely recognized the source.

## Important

Do not search the image online during the task.

At the end, you may be asked whether any source looked familiar and what you thought the study was testing.

The study does not require you to agree with any archaeological or historical interpretation.
