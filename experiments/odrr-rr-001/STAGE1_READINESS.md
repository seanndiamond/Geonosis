## Court finding at this stage

The project has progressed from an exploratory observation to a **testable confirmatory protocol with executable analysis**, but it has **not** reached independent reproduction.

The blocking items are upstream and concrete: exact stimuli/provenance, matched controls, ethics/research governance, blind-key sealing, external review and formal preregistration.

No claim of peer review, replication or survival is authorized by this readiness record.
