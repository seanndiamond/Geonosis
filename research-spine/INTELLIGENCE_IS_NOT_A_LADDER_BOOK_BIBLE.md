## 1. Fixed architecture and story promise

- Preserve the **prologue, introduction and twelve-chapter structure**. Do not move, merge or expand chapters without explicit author approval.
- Central proposition: **Intelligence is not a ladder.** Distinct beings and systems contribute differentiated capacities; task performance is not an all-purpose ranking of worth, legitimate authority or relational competence.
- Open with Yuval Noah Harari's “cows of the future” warning. Keep the risk of living under opaque, uncorrectable arrangements serious. The book's contribution is a grounded investigation of participation, restraint, judgment, accountability and human authority, not a caricature of Harari.
- Donny Brook, Sean's German Shepherd, is a **living participant in the inquiry**, not a decorative metaphor.
- The ancient archive is an investigated source of visual relationships and practical questions, not merely artwork illustrating a preselected AI theory.
- Final scene and final line are protected: **“Donny is taking me for a walk.”** Do not disclose or paraphrase the final revelation prematurely in the narrative.
- The publisher-acquisitions review supplied in chat was a **Gemini AI simulation**, positive about the manuscript's hybrid voice, tactile stories and commercial positioning. It was not an actual publisher offer. Its principal caution was cognitive whiplash from abrupt disciplinary transitions.

## 2. Voice and editorial practice

1. Conversational, confident, observant, investigative. Let a person hear a human mind at work.
2. Lead with the **scene, object, animal, decision or original image** rather than with abstract exposition. Explain technical concepts as the encounter demands.
3. Prefer connected, variable read-aloud cadence. Avoid stringing together dramatic single-line conclusions, repetitive rhetorical questions or generic academic padding.
4. Reduce repetitive first person except where Sean's firsthand action, observation, decision or responsibility supplies the evidence.
5. Preserve dry, earned humour (the milk errand, Donny's bibliography, the footy, the shoe, corporate over-management). Do not manufacture jokes at a serious human injury.
6. Avoid recurring AI tics, including “that matters,” “extraordinary,” “profound consequences,” “it is worth noting,” “in this context,” and inflated claims.
7. Present source observations, transformations, proposed visual readings and practical returns **clearly and distinctly**. Do not insert apologetic disclaimers merely because the readings are unconventional; qualify at the exact point the evidence requires.
8. Do not substitute conventional catalogue labels for an image-first analysis; likewise do not silently present a working pictographic interpretation as independently established historical translation.
9. Never misrepresent OGI as a completed, demonstrated general-intelligence system. It is an active research program whose five investigated functions are **alignment, cohesion, understanding, representation and return**.
10. Keep images alongside the prose they substantiate, not gathered into a decorative gallery disconnected from the claims.
11. Protect narrative suspense. A chapter may promise a later investigation but must not spend its central discovery early.
12. Retain author-approved anecdotes and precise corrections; do not reintroduce discarded alternative explanations of Donny's decision as a puppy.
13. When a genuine correction is needed, update this record **and** the relevant publication manuscript; neither alone is sufficient.

## 6. Research and epistemic distinctions

Keep four layers separate in the narrative and underlying evidence:
1. **Source:** artifact, image, documented firsthand observation or event record.
2. **Derivation:** what is visibly traced, compared, rotated, mirrored or otherwise inferred.
3. **Working interpretation:** relation/function proposed by the author's image-first analysis.
4. **Return:** independently observable later change, comparison, correction or practical application.

The **Return Test** can be practical (the chair turning towards Donny; attention to cat/dog interactions), cross-image, or referential (AI correcting a fused artifact identity after returning to the records). A useful return is evidence about an inquiry's consequences and correction pathway, not automatic proof of an ancient translation.

**Court of Provenance:** original artifact → observable marks → explicit transformations/comparisons → proposed meaning → challenge, correction and repeatable inspection.

**OGI:** A program of relational AI governance/research. Do not claim unrun tests passed, reduced energy/computation or established consciousness. Growing freedom/permissions is considered in proportion to demonstrated relational competence, monitoring and accountability; neither permanent confinement nor unconstrained autonomy is the thesis.

## 7. Change-control procedure

When a chapter is proposed for lock:
1. Read its **full** working source and most recent proposed revision.
2. Present the complete read-aloud prose, retaining its distinctive stories and source details.
3. Obtain author's explicit approval.
4. Mark prose **LOCKED** with date and version. Do not silently adjust locked text in later exports.
5. Separate **image review**, provenance/rights check, whole-book editorial continuity and print proof from prose approval.
6. Update this bible's status and decisions. Link stable manuscript file/commit when available.

Before adding a new thread, ask:
- Which existing chapter genuinely needs it?
- Does it **replace**, **deepen** or **merely repeat** what is already present?
- Does it reveal an ending or a later discovery too soon?
- Does it return to the cow, dog, archive, craft or institutional responsibility in a way the reader can experience?
- Is the underlying source available and correctly represented?

**The architecture stays. Put the reasons for the temple on its walls.**
