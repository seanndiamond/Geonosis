# The Girl in the Clay and Civilizational Cycles

Research handover, 21 November 2026.

## Research sources and interpretation

Metropolitan Museum of Art tablet 11.217.19 is catalogued as a receipt of lambs. The Geonosis reading uses rotation, mirroring and offset overlays and proposes a sequence concerning a young person’s education and relationships. The image reading, hypotheses about past events and the novel’s development are recorded at distinct evidentiary levels.

## Research questions carried forward

- How do individual decisions enter institutions that can amplify, restrain or correct them?
- How can the capacities developed through education be directed toward different ends?
- How does protective law operate under political pressure?
- How can a model distinguish external destruction from the internal loss of corrective capacity?
- How do preservation, interpretive recoverability and public transmission affect what survives?

## Research questions / next tests
- Can we document image-by-image derivations separating visible marks, transformations, interpretive claims and fictive additions?
- Which source-frame relations support individual education, elephant cooperation, assignment, custody and rescue specifically? Which were supplied as captions or imagined transitions?
- Can any reading be falsified by returns, incompatible sequencing, alternate original orientations or independent interpreters?
- Does institutional failure of correction predict loss of coherence better than hazard, resource shortage, conquest or organizational complexity alone?
- Which modern historical cases distinguish an institution's nominal legal protections from their actual operation under political threat?
- What is the evidentiary pathway from preserved object to proposed civilizational instruction?
