## Method result
- **Field before form**; do not accept labels in place of derivations.
- Preserve the continuous image sequence and local lexicon.
- Record chronological origin of each working interpretation and later returns.
- Keep failed interpretations and explicit supersessions rather than silently deleting them.
- A confident narrative chapter and a separately auditable evidence ledger serve different purposes.
- Do not 'prove' a thesis by weakening or changing the original question.

## Public archive boundary
