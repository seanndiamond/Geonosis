## Core distinctions
- **Many civilizations, not one synchronized global civilization.** Shared trade, technology, catastrophe or symbolic forms do not establish a single homogeneous political/cultural society.
- **Relational capability versus imposed identity.** The Ra crystalline-pyramid model enables a civilization's own potential; the claimant's pyramid imposes what a ruling authority wants it to become. These are Geonosis interpretive propositions.
- **Correction rather than faultlessness.** Coherence is expressed through effective protection, accountability, repair and adaptive coordination, not the absence of all mistakes or disagreement.
- **External versus internal breakdown.** A physically destroyed regional system can be internally coherent; institutions can fail morally and organizationally while infrastructure survives.
- **People versus autonomous system.** Descendants, knowledge and traditions may survive the end of an independent civilizational order.
- **Research interpretation versus fiction.** The three-centimetre tablet and its overlays are evidence-bearing research material. Elataya's pictographic reading is a working interpretation. *The Girl in the Clay* elaborates that reading with invented Veyra, scenes and dialogue; its fictional additions are not independent historical findings.
