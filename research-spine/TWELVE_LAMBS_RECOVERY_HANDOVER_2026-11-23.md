## Current character canon (author-led refinements, 22 Nov)
**Susan Elliott:** diligent, kind, gentle and professionally excellent; enjoys more intelligent people's thought and the humour arising from it without competitiveness. Comfortable in friendships/work and small pleasures, especially lunch with Mum and riverside walks. Not primarily seeking romance. Recognises volatile Jim and disciplined Mark without trying to reform them. Brown eyes; a smile that makes Jim glad to return. Increasing attachment to Elataya leads her mother to ask genuine questions about ancient girl and Susan's feelings. After Veyra's destruction, Nima becomes a daughter-like relationship organically through shared work, without possessive or sentimental speeches.

**Jim Climham:** expert, passionately committed archaeologist and hard-working disciplined field leader; emotionally reactive, often indignant, defensive about perceived humiliation, partly shaped by father's hurtful brush/masculinity association. **Jim does not have analytical self-awareness of this psychology**; do not put therapy-like thoughts in him or narrator explanations. He sees teammates as professional coworkers, not close companions, and after seven weeks enjoys returning to city, machines, and especially emotional anchor Susan, whom he genuinely loves platonically. This does NOT motivate his archaeological profession or manufacture of finds. Helicopter has no pilot and empty seats, which he instinctively turns to share sights with. Jim respects Mark professionally, then deeper emotionally after Mark delivers blunt, accurate non-contemptuous rebuke.

**Mark Whaler:** comfortable upper-middle-class upbringing, excellent education, materially secure; intellectual, technical, reserved, measured, calm, slightly aristocratic sense of entitlement but not consciously superior. Bonsai, clean precision cycling gear, maintained kayak, river touring. Relaxed face often resembles a smile, which Jim misreads as put-down or flirtation; Susan knows better. Doesn't seek banter or sarcasm; tolerates anger poorly and withdraws rather than escalating. Finds Jim's domestic grievance at work professionally bewildering. He can genuinely laugh at the absurdity of Jim lowering a loudspeaker to downstairs neighbour; asks precise practical questions (which music, how loud, how lowered), then bluntly tells Jim he's being crazy and to sort his shit out so they can work. No endless laughter or talk after rebuke. His non-contemptuous integrity earns Jim's deeper respect. A kayaking phone call leads Jim to offer spare fishing kayak; Mark realises invitation, agrees genuinely out of curiosity. Fishing trip can reveal growing trust without sentimental confessions.

**Susan's mother:** pragmatic, affectionate, curious about her daughter, not an oracle/philosopher. She asks what is interesting about Elataya and how old the tablet really is; Jim's approximate 4,000 year date may reflect deposition/context, not manufacture. It could have belonged to earlier archives, no demonstrated alternative date. Mother is interested in why Susan is moved by a long-dead girl, not in announcing “you're attached” or producing cascade of wise lines.

**Nima:** enters in the *new city after Veyra's destruction*, not in earlier Veyra chapters. Young, emotionally engaged, persistent, self-inviting, actually contributes to tablet investigations without salary or request. Sees institutional hurdles as procedural solvable tasks, takes legitimate ecological/duck concerns seriously, gets approvals rather than arguing. May intuit Susan's distress and organise folders without speaking; but don't gag her either: human dialogue must have genuine reason. Becomes scientist and may receive custody of tablet from Susan later in life. Susan/Nima arc is daughter-like but not melodramatic.
