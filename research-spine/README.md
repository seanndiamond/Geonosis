# Geonosis Public Research Spine

This directory is the public map of the five-paper Geonosis research spine. The papers test the same governing question from different directions: **can visible structure be audited before inherited explanation is allowed to dominate the field?**

## 1. Beyond Phonetics

**Title:** *Méthodê Glyph, Picterpretation, and Geonosis as a Falsifiable Operator-First Method for Visual Knowledge Systems*

**Function:** Canon method paper. It defines the rules before the controversial case studies arrive.

**Status:** Publication-ready preprint V1.0.

**Core contribution:** Operator-first audit; evidence ladder; claim tiering; failure conditions; AI-assisted audit procedure; public-claim standards; terrain and visual-system protocols.

**Repository record:** [`01-beyond-phonetics.md`](01-beyond-phonetics.md)

---

## 2. If the Administrative Reading Cannot Account for the Field

**Function:** Visible-derivation audit of cuneiform and related inherited script interpretations.

**Status:** Active research programme. The updated paper is not being presented here as finished publication because the public archive still requires a consolidated case record and derivation ledger.

**Core question:** Can an accepted administrative or phonetic reading be demonstrated from the visible artefact through an explicit artefact → sign → value → grammar → meaning chain?

**Repository record:** [`02-cuneiform-visible-derivation-audit.md`](02-cuneiform-visible-derivation-audit.md)

---

## 3. From Egg-Field to Womb-Field

**Subtitle:** *An Operator-First Reading of Taş Tepeler as a Neolithic Grammar of Generation, Embodiment, and Social Reproduction*

**Function:** Regional archaeological test of whether apparently separate forms behave as a constrained reproductive operator grammar.

**Status:** Working APA/claim-tier draft. Image provenance, permissions, source labels, and chronology checks remain publication gates.

**Repository record:** [`03-tas-tepeler-status.md`](03-tas-tepeler-status.md)

---

## 4. Seeing Meaning Early

**Title:** *Pattern Recognition, Attachment, and Emotional Development in Children Aged 0–4*

**Function:** Cognitive foundation for the claim that pattern recognition is a real human faculty requiring discipline rather than reflex dismissal.

**Status:** Complete research paper / submission-ready research output.

**Important boundary:** Evidence that humans detect meaningful patterns does **not** validate a particular archaeological or terrain reading. It establishes capacity, not correctness.

**Repository record:** [`04-seeing-meaning-early.md`](04-seeing-meaning-early.md)

---

## 5. From Glucose to Bone

**Subtitle:** *Atomo-Spiritual Evolution, Field Before Form, and the Body as a Living Archive*

**Function:** Biological extension of the framework from terrain and artefact to molecular and anatomical structure.

**Status:** Developed preprint draft. Scientific background, Geonosis interpretation, and higher-risk metaphysical extension are explicitly separated.

**Repository record:** [`05-from-glucose-to-bone.md`](05-from-glucose-to-bone.md)

---

## Publication rule

A place in the five-paper spine does not automatically mean “published.” Each record states its actual status. The repository distinguishes complete papers, developed preprints, active programmes, and working drafts so that chronology and evidentiary maturity remain visible rather than retrofitted later.
