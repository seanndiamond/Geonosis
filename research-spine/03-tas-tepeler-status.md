# From Egg-Field to Womb-Field

## An Operator-First Reading of Taş Tepeler as a Neolithic Grammar of Generation, Embodiment, and Social Reproduction

**Sean Diamond | Geonosis Project**

**Status:** Working APA/claim-tier draft. Not yet represented here as a final publication file.

## Working thesis

The Taş Tepeler complex may preserve not isolated fertility symbols but a staged visual-spatial grammar of generation, including field, boundary, seed, cavity, body, emergence, nourishment, and social integration.

The working regional model examines whether distinct sites preserve different operators within a larger sequence. Candidate roles include many-potential or egg-cluster fields, donor fields, trait negotiation, womb-fields, emergence architecture, household body-fields, and modular instruction-fields.

## Method boundary

The project deliberately separates:

1. **archaeological observation** — visible forms, plans, reliefs, cavities, pillars, gestures, site relations, and published context;
2. **operator interpretation** — proposed functions derived from constrained visual behaviour;
3. **grammar hypothesis** — a regional sequence linking operators across sites;
4. **speculative extension** — biological engineering, managed hybridisation, consciousness-field, or related higher-risk interpretations.

Tier 4 material is not foundational evidence for the Tier 2 or Tier 3 claims.

## Failure conditions

The thesis weakens or fails if:

- proposed operators appear randomly rather than in constrained contexts;
- chronology contradicts the proposed sequence;
- standard archaeological explanations account for the distribution more simply;
- image sources are misidentified, manipulated, or incorrectly attributed;
- claimed recurrences disappear under independent review;
- proposed operator mapping cannot be reproduced by observers who know the method but not the expected answer.

## Publication gates still open

The current draft explicitly requires:

- verified image sources;
- rights or permission review where required;
- final source links and captions;
- chronology verification;
- confirmation of site labels;
- final citation audit.

For that reason, GitHub presently exposes the research logic and its falsification gates rather than presenting the working manuscript as a finished public paper.
