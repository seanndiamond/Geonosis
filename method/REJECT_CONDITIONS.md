# Canonical Reject Conditions

These conditions are intended to stop a preferred reading from surviving simply because the researcher likes it.

## RC-01 — Self-authorising structure
Authority or validity is asserted by the same element it is meant to authorise.

**Response:** FAIL, or narrow the claim until independent structure is available.

## RC-02 — Collapsed roles
Operator, validator, recorder, recipient, or another necessary role collapses without loss of supposed function.

**Response:** FAIL the executable-system claim.

## RC-03 — Unmediated state transition
A state change is asserted without a visible mediating operator, instrument, boundary, or step.

**Response:** FAIL the transition claim or return INCONCLUSIVE.

## RC-04 — Boundary has no effect
Frames or boundaries behave decoratively; crossing or ignoring them has no detectable consequence.

**Response:** FAIL the boundary-dependent grammar claim.

## RC-05 — Narrative completion required
Coherence requires inventing missing operators, steps, motives, or history.

**Response:** return INCONCLUSIVE and preserve the gap.

## RC-06 — Scale incoherence
Structure breaks, inverts, or depends on a fragile scale without justification.

**Response:** FAIL or reduce the scale and claim scope.

## RC-07 — Isolation is not a system
An isolated token lacks adjacency or series evidence needed to demonstrate flow, mediation, auditability, or transition.

**Response:** register as OPEN OBSERVATION or triage only.

## RC-08 — Non-falsifiable reading
No clear failure condition can be stated and the interpretation survives every possible counter-test.

**Response:** do not proceed.

# Anti-pareidolia gates

Calling something pareidolia is not itself an analysis. High-risk visual claims are tested through:

1. **Internal structure** — does the apparent form contain relational detail appropriate to the reading?
2. **Constraint** — what features make alternatives less likely?
3. **Recurrence** — does the same operator or grammar recur?
4. **Scale** — does structure survive justified scale changes?
5. **Prediction** — does the reading predict features before inspection?
6. **Independent return** — can another viewer recover the central observation without heavy coaching?
7. **Alternative model** — does a geological, compositional, cultural, biological or random model explain the field more economically?
8. **Failure** — what observation would defeat the reading?

The same audit applies in both directions. Conventional readings do not receive immunity because they are conventional; Geonosis readings do not receive leniency because they are unconventional.
