That amendment develops the Geonosis reading that some apparent wall damage may be candidate evidence of responsive-stone loss of coherence; that pyramid function can be deactivated by removing operating information rather than merely demolishing masonry; and that the surviving technological grammar may remain visible while the knowledge needed to operate it is severed.

## Central reconstruction

The paper's current material spine is:

> **coherent mineral body → field-induced dissociation → carrier-managed responsive body → field-maintained responsiveness → spatial address → selective or whole-body crystallisation → integration**

The current information spine is:

> **consciousness → image → field → matter → durable form**

The paper treats the Tabernacle as a portable or scalable civilisational operating architecture, not first as a later religious object.
