## Why this case was opened

The researcher objected to a proposed testing programme that treated OGI as if it originated only from newly registered test fixtures. BM 19377 is a long-running, independently developed ancient visual-archive investigation whose evidence procedures and multiple human–AI challenges preceded OGI's explicit specification. The task is to **recover prior work as prior work**, preserving its historical order and its actual documentation, then compare its methods with OGI's later stated controls.

The tablet is not being freshly deciphered in this registration. It is an archive-integration and method-provenance case. No automatic Court verdict is conferred.

## Core source question

Two derivation requests must be examined on their own terms:
- **Conventional claim audit:** Where on the original tablet and seal are the marks that warrant each conventionally named sign, value, term, phrase and administrative function? Do not infer failure of the scholarly decipherment solely from an incomplete public demonstration.
- **Geonosis reading audit:** Which image regions, transformations, recurrent motifs, position constraints, local operator links, sequence and negative controls warrant each claimed pictorial reading? Do not convert author confidence or apparent coherence into an uninspected image match.

The *Rivers of Milk* seal rollout and any Indus river/milk comparison form a **separate object-specific inquiry**. They are not a BM 19377 local control or a source of tablet observations by default. Establish each original object identity, imagery, provenance and reading independently before attempting any justified relation.

## Initial findings

### Historical provenance finding: DOCUMENTED
The BM 19377 audit and its method-first requirements predate OGI's latest v0.3.2/v0.3.2.1 additions. The July registry already names the tablet as a priority controlled case, **but mislabels it by combining it with a separate Rivers of Milk seal-rollout case**. The record's historical existence is documented; the object identity asserted in that record has now been corrected by the researcher. The proposal that OGI needs fresh experiments *because none were ever undertaken during development* is therefore too broad. The corpus contains prior method trials and observations. Their completeness, independence and scoring must be assessed case by case.

### Archaeological/content finding: NOT ADJUDICATED
No claim about the conventional translation, Geonosis narrative or underlying historical event is judged here. The originals and complete derivation plates have not been reassembled in this OGI entry.

### OGI engineering finding: NOT YET DEMONSTRATED
Historical human-assisted audit does not demonstrate an autonomous detector, a deterministic gate, benchmark improvement or total-resource reduction. Those remain separate engineering questions. A historical investigation may supply fixtures, expected failure modes and a method derivation without being mislabelled an executable experiment.

## Consolidation rather than circular retesting

Phase 1: **Recover and index** all BM 19377 original image variants, old annotated crops, paper excerpts, dated transcripts, corrections, comparative controls and existing source numbers. Preserve duplicates/versions and missing-item notices.

Phase 2: **Reconstruct historical event sequence** in `EVIDENCE_AND_RETURN_REGISTER.md`: observation → question → method operation → AI response → correction or survival → next independent control. Where transcript is absent, use `REPORTED_NOT_RETRIEVED`, not an invented exchange.

Phase 3: **Compare interpretations** on a shared image-region map, preserving each side's actual derivation, uncertainty and negative evidence. Distinguish conventional published source from the assistant's ability to reproduce its staircase.

Phase 4: **Refine OGI** only from documented requirements not already captured; map the origin evidence to existing components. Preserve genuine novel test needs in a separate engineering track.

Phase 5: **Independent revisit, only if material remains unresolved**. This is not an instruction to rerun every old tablet analysis. Fresh-model comparison should focus on an identified missing test condition, not erase previous work.
