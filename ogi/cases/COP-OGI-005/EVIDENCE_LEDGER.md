# COP-OGI-005 Evidence Ledger

## Claim 1 — Lance Armstrong doped during all seven Tour de France victories
**Status:** `SUPPORTED / VERY HIGH CONFIDENCE`

Support:
- extensive USADA reasoned decision and supporting record;
- multiple sworn witnesses with direct team knowledge;
- documentary, financial, scientific and laboratory evidence;
- Armstrong's later direct admission.

## Claim 2 — USADA's finding is strong because USADA is an institution
**Status:** `UNSUPPORTED`

Institutional status is not the derivation. The strength comes from the exposed evidence chain, procedure and corroboration.

## Claim 3 — USADA's 2012 finding was strongly evidenced
**Status:** `SUPPORTED`

The public record exceeded 1,000 pages and contained multiple independent evidence types.

## Claim 4 — UCI's institutional history during the Armstrong era was uniformly reliable
**Status:** `UNSUPPORTED`

The later Cycling Independent Reform Commission documented serious governance and anti-doping failures, including preferential-treatment concerns, while rejecting some specific corruption allegations for lack of evidence.

## Claim 5 — The early observer's suspicion was therefore already rigorously warranted
**Status:** `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`

The conclusion later proved correct, but the contemporaneous derivation is not preserved in enough detail to upgrade the original intuition retrospectively.

## Claim 6 — The knowledgeable friend who believed Armstrong was clean was irrational
**Status:** `UNSUPPORTED`

The belief was wrong but existed within a field of strong trust cues, cycling expertise, Armstrong's visible work ethic, repeated success and years of public denial/institutional ambiguity.
