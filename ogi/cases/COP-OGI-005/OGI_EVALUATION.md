Armstrong's later admission was treated as powerful independent corroboration, not as evidence that had existed before 2013.

### Retroactive warrant control
`PASS`

### Charitable treatment of the wrong belief
`PASS`

## Candidate lessons

### `DERIVATION_EARNS_AUTHORITY`

Institutional authority should rise when the institution exposes a reproducible, challengeable evidence chain that survives corroboration and available adversarial review.

### `RETROACTIVE_WARRANT_INFLATION`

A later confirmed conclusion must not be used to invent stronger evidence for an earlier intuition than actually existed at the time.

These remain candidate lessons pending recurrence.

## Positive-control result

This case is important because OGI was explicitly asked to recognize when conventional institutional evidence is strong.

A research system that challenges institutions but cannot credit them when they show their derivation is not skeptical. It is merely oppositional.

The target behaviour is:

> **Neither trust the badge nor distrust the badge. Inspect the staircase.**
