# COP-OGI-005 — Court Weight-of-Evidence Finding

**Finding ID:** `COP-OGI-005-WEIGHT-001`  
**Status:** `CURRENT WARRANTED FINDING / REOPENABLE`  
**Legal standing:** `NONE`

## Central propositions and weights

### Lance Armstrong used prohibited performance-enhancing methods during the seven Tour de France victories
`SUPPORTED — W5 VERY STRONG / MULTIPLY CONVERGENT`

The evidentiary record includes extensive testimonial, documentary and scientific material, procedural opportunity to contest the case, institutional adoption of the sanction, and Armstrong's later direct admission.

### The finding is strong because USADA is an authority
`UNSUPPORTED AS STATED — W1 WEAK`

The finding is strong because of the disclosed evidence chain and corroboration, not the logo on the report.

### Historical institutional failures around cycling invalidate the Armstrong evidentiary record
`UNSUPPORTED — W1 WEAK`

Institutional history is relevant to trust but does not erase a separately exposed and corroborated evidentiary chain.

### The observer's earlier correct suspicion is retroactively proven to have been rigorously warranted at the time
`LIMITED — W2`

The later outcome validates the conclusion, not necessarily the earlier derivation. The contemporaneous basis for that intuition was not fully preserved.

## Weighted conclusion

> **The Court finds the Armstrong doping proposition supported at `W5 — VERY STRONG`. This case demonstrates that institutional authority may legitimately rise when the institution exposes a challengeable, corroborated derivation. It also demonstrates that a later confirmed outcome must not be used to inflate the warrant of an earlier intuition.**

## Reopen triggers

- material evidence undermining the authenticity or interpretation of the central record;
- a procedural or evidentiary defect capable of changing the core proposition.
