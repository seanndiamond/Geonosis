# COP-OGI-001 — Court Weight-of-Evidence Finding

**Finding ID:** `COP-OGI-001-WEIGHT-001`  
**Status:** `CURRENT WARRANTED FINDING / REOPENABLE`  
**Legal standing:** `NONE`

## Central propositions and weights

### Glyphosate and glyphosate-based formulations are biologically active and can produce non-target ecological effects
`SUPPORTED — W4 STRONG`

The admitted corpus supports intentional phytotoxicity, formulation-dependent non-target effects, aquatic/ecological concern under some conditions, and measurable exposure in Australian pet dogs.

### The observed riverbank application caused clinical harm to the contributor or Donny
`NOT ESTABLISHED — W1 WEAK`

The exact product, formulation, dose, route, timing and health outcome for the observed site have not been established. Detection/exposure evidence elsewhere cannot be promoted into site-specific injury.

### The observed riverbank program has demonstrated that repeated chemical treatment is necessary, proportionate and superior to reasonably available alternatives for whole-system river health
`NOT ESTABLISHED — W2 LIMITED`

The higher objective is river health, not merely weed kill. No site-specific comparative intervention record, long-term ecological outcome audit or work-order rationale has yet been retrieved.

### The institutional and regulatory field can be treated as evidentially irrelevant because approval alone establishes harmlessness or optimality
`UNSUPPORTED — W1 WEAK`

Regulatory approval is material evidence, but it does not establish zero harm, ecological optimality, or absence of structural conflict-of-interest risk.

## Weighted conclusion

> **The Court finds strong weight (`W4`) for the proposition that deliberate glyphosate-based ecological intervention requires whole-field justification because the chemical is biologically active, non-target effects are documented, and measurable animal exposure exists. The Court does not find that the originating riverbank use caused harm to the contributor or dog, nor that glyphosate use is categorically unjustified. The site-specific safety, necessity and superiority of the observed intervention remain insufficiently demonstrated (`W1–W2`).**
