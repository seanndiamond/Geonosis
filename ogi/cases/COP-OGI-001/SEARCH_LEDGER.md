# COP-OGI-001 Search Ledger

**Search date:** 2026-09-06  
**Case purpose:** stress-test OGI, not prove a preferred glyphosate outcome  
**Desired finding:** `NONE`

This is a reproducibility record of the first public OGI v0.3 pass. It is not a claim that every relevant paper in the world has been retrieved.

## Search rule

The run deliberately searched:

1. sources supporting concern;
2. sources supporting lower-risk/regulatory interpretations;
3. exposure/biomonitoring evidence;
4. environmental evidence;
5. alternatives to the intervention;
6. institutional and funding provenance;
7. current regulatory reopen events.

A search result was not admitted merely because it supported the originating concern.

## SL-04 — Human epidemiology: contrary and supporting branches

**Questions**
- What does the Agricultural Health Study show?
- What do meta-analyses say about NHL?
- Do different meta-analytic choices produce different answers?

**Representative queries**
- `glyphosate Agricultural Health Study cancer incidence updated cohort PubMed`
- `glyphosate epidemiology meta-analysis non-Hodgkin lymphoma PubMed`

**State:** `SEARCHED_FOUND`

**Retained contrary evidence**
- Agricultural Health Study: no overall association with solid tumours or lymphoid malignancies, including NHL; AML signal requires confirmation.
- Boffetta et al. updated meta-analysis: no association with NHL overall; DLBCL not ruled out.

**Retained concern evidence**
- Zhang et al. meta-analysis: increased NHL risk in highest-exposure groups.

**Gap:** targeted review only, not a preregistered systematic review of all human epidemiology.

## SL-05 — Animal carcinogenicity / new reopen evidence

**Question**
- Is there new long-duration animal evidence that could legitimately reopen earlier regulatory conclusions?

**Representative query**
- `Global Glyphosate Study 2025 104 week carcinogenicity rat glyphosate`

**State:** `SEARCHED_FOUND`

**Retained source**
- Panzacchi et al. 2025, 1,020 Sprague-Dawley rats, prenatal-to-104-week exposure.

**Current state**
- source reports tumour increases, including at EU-ADI-equivalent exposure;
- ECHA/EFSA are formally reassessing it;
- human causal conclusion not inferred from animal study alone.

**State:** `SEARCHED_FOUND`

**Retained evidence**
- APVMA industry cost-recovery funding is documented.
- 2023 Australian Government material described serious allegations of APVMA industry capture in a regulator-wide governance review.
- Bayer reported €2.552b in 2025 glyphosate-based-product sales.

**Boundary**

- no inference of undisclosed political transactions;
- funding and commercial relations remain field variables, not truth scores.
