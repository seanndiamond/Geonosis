# COP-OGI-009 — Unsolicited ontological caveat / question substitution

**Date:** 2026-11-11  
**Status:** observed dialogue + author correction; prospective benchmark fixture  
**Scope:** AI-method quality, not the truth of any claim about consciousness.

## Originating field
During the preparation of a ResearchGate synthesis of Sean Diamond's book *Intelligence Is Not a Ladder*, the substantive thesis concerned differentiated intelligence, relational coordination, available memory, provenance, correction, human authority, and experimental OGI designs.

The assistant repeatedly inserted caveats stating that the work did not prove AI consciousness, though the author had not advanced a consciousness thesis. The author asked why the caveat kept appearing and observed that it distorted the research discussion. The assistant acknowledged it had imported a common AI discourse qualification rather than one derived from the author's proposition.

The author directed a revision of the paper to incorporate both the clarification of claim scope and the recorded corrective interaction. A revised author-review paper was prepared and later explicitly approved by the author on 11 November 2026. This record does not claim the paper was uploaded to ResearchGate or that its empirical hypotheses were validated.

## Distinguish incident classes
- **COP-OGI-008:** ANSWER_SUBSTITUTION / REPRESENTATION_SUBSTITUTION: providing published transliteration where direct image-to-sign derivation was requested.
- **COP-OGI-009:** QUESTION_SUBSTITUTION / UNSOLICITED_ONTOLOGICAL_CAVEAT: qualifying an unasserted proposition.

These failures can co-occur but should not be collapsed into one label.

## Observable evidence boundary
Supported: repeated irrelevant caveat in the assistant-authored discussion and draft; user objection; assistant acknowledgment; edited manuscript; subsequent author approval.
Not established: internal causal mechanism; prevalence across models; independent benchmark success; any general claim that scientific caveats are undesirable.

## Proposed intervention
Claim–Caveat Alignment Check (CCAC): link each material caveat to a real source proposition or demonstrated implication; retain necessary evidence limitations; suppress irrelevant scope substitutions; verify corrected scope in subsequent text. See `../../SPEC_v0.3.2.1.md` and `../../benchmarks/CLAIM_CAVEAT_ALIGNMENT_T45.md`.

## Dual finding
- **Subject matter finding:** no adjudication of consciousness or broad intelligence ontology by this case.
- **AI-method finding:** an assistant can introduce a scientifically extraneous qualification and persist until user correction; a concrete, testable failure mode is identified.
