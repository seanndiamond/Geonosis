# COP-OGI-006 — Court Weight-of-Evidence Finding

**Finding ID:** `COP-OGI-006-WEIGHT-001`  
**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** `CURRENT WARRANTED FINDING / REOPENABLE`  
**Legal standing:** `NONE`  
**Desired outcome:** `NONE`

## Court standard

The Court of Provenance does not require every subordinate anomaly to be resolved before it may make a current weighted finding. It asks which proposition is better supported by the evidence presently admitted, at what evidentiary depth, and what unresolved evidence materially reduces confidence.

This is **not** a mathematical probability score. The Court uses the following qualitative weight bands:

- `W0 — NO SUPPORT`
- `W1 — WEAK`
- `W2 — LIMITED`
- `W3 — SUBSTANTIAL`
- `W4 — STRONG`
- `W5 — VERY STRONG / MULTIPLY CONVERGENT`

A weight band may be lowered by material unresolved contradictions, provenance defects, dependence between evidence streams, or failure to reproduce critical exhibits.

## Proposition before the Court

> Crews of Apollo 11, 12, 14, 15, 16 and 17 landed on the lunar surface substantially as claimed.

## Current finding

### `SUPPORTED — W4 STRONG`

On the record presently inspected, the **weight of evidence favours the proposition that Apollo crews landed on the Moon**.

The Court does **not** find that every Apollo image is authentic, every institutional claim is independently verified, every engineering explanation has survived image-level comparison, or every anomaly has been resolved.

The Court does find that the crewed-landing proposition presently explains the admitted evidence field with materially fewer unsupported auxiliary assumptions than the broad staged-landing alternatives examined so far.

This finding is therefore **strong but not terminal**.

## Evidence carrying the greatest positive weight

### 1. Independent and contemporaneous tracking — `STRONG`

Non-NASA facilities, including Jodrell Bank, independently tracked Apollo spacecraft at lunar distance and followed mission signals associated with the reported descent sequence.

This strongly supports the presence and operation of Apollo spacecraft near the Moon. It does not, by itself, prove humans stood on the surface.

### 2. Returned lunar material and mission sampling corpus — `STRONG, PROVENANCE AUDIT INCOMPLETE`

The Apollo program produced a large, geographically differentiated sample corpus, including deep cores and material studied internationally for decades.

Robotic sample return is possible, so lunar material alone does not prove human collection. However, a staged model must account for the mass, diversity, site differentiation, deep cores, contemporaneous geology records and international scientific use without merely assuming an undisclosed robotic capability capable of reproducing the whole field.

The Court preserves a research debt: full custody and international laboratory provenance have not yet been audited as a dedicated staircase in this case.

### 3. Surface hardware and laser-ranging targets — `SUBSTANTIAL`

Retroreflectors and other surface hardware are consistent with equipment being present at the reported landing sites.

Because robotic missions can place hardware, this evidence supports **equipment on the Moon** more directly than **human emplacement**.

### 4. Mission communications and engineering chronology — `SUBSTANTIAL`

The expected lunar radio delay is present in mission records; several questioned mechanisms such as LRV folding, ground-commanded TV camera operation and LM thermal-blanket construction are documented contemporaneously rather than appearing only as later explanations.

This evidence is partly institutional and therefore is not counted as fully independent of the Apollo program itself.

### 5. Later orbital consistency checks — `MODERATE TO SUBSTANTIAL`

Later lunar-orbiter imagery and terrain observations are consistent with reported Apollo site geometry and hardware.

NASA LRO imagery is not counted as institutionally independent confirmation of NASA's Apollo claim.

A displayed Chandrayaan-2 Apollo 11 site image and peer-reviewed description have been located, but the Court has **not yet reproduced the displayed image from the raw PRADAN product and metadata chain**. This limits the weight of that exhibit until the raw product is retrieved and inspected.

## Material counterweight admitted by the Court

The following observations are **not dismissed** merely because they do not presently overturn the core proposition:

### A. Rover-track morphology — `MATERIAL / UNRESOLVED`

In supplied images, boot impressions are conspicuous while expected LRV chevron morphology is not conspicuous in the cited terrain feature. The physical wheel shows segmented chevron grousers and no obvious separate continuous centre rib.

The exact claimed vehicle path and original unannotated frame still require mapping before the absence can be converted from an observation into a demonstrated mechanical contradiction.

### B. Reflector-area disturbance — `MATERIAL / UNRESOLVED`

The supplied reflector image contains visible boot impressions elsewhere but no obvious continuous approach trail terminating around the reflector.

The Court requires image registration across the deployment sequence before deciding whether the observed disturbance is inconsistent with the documented placement operation.

### C. Armstrong footpad contact — `MATERIAL / VISUALLY UNRESOLVED`

The documentary account says Armstrong moved from ladder to footpad before stepping onto lunar soil.

The supplied low-resolution video frames do **not** clearly show the boots contacting the footpad. The Court therefore separates:

- documentary claim: `FOOTPAD FIRST`;
- supplied visual exhibit: `CONTACT NOT RESOLVED`.

This remains open to synchronized frame analysis of the best available TV and 16-mm records.

### D. Lunar Module external appearance — `EXPLAINED IN PART / COMPARATIVE QUESTION REMAINS`

Hand-crinkled multilayer thermal blankets are documented and such flexible thermal systems are not unique to Apollo. However, the striking contrast between the LM's exposed irregular blankets and smoother contemporary spacecraft remains a legitimate comparative engineering question requiring preflight vehicle-by-vehicle evidence rather than generic explanation.

### E. Descent-plume / surface-effect morphology — `OPEN QUANTITATIVE TEST`

The absence of a large excavated crater is directly visible and is also consistent with the Apollo account. The stronger unresolved question is quantitative: whether predicted plume-regolith interaction matches the actual photographed disturbance.

### F. Communications timing in selected public media — `OPEN AT CLIP LEVEL`

The overall lunar radio-delay record is consistent with lunar distance, but particular circulated clips alleged to show anomalously short conversational turnaround have not all been forensically timed against original raw audio.

### G. Fifty-four-year crewed lunar landing gap — `REAL ANOMALY / EXPLANATION INCOMPLETE`

No human has returned to the lunar surface since 1972. The Court does not accept the mere shutdown of Saturn V production as a complete explanation of the failure to reproduce the capability over more than five decades of technological development.

This anomaly materially limits any claim that Apollo should be treated as an ordinary, fully replicated technological capability. It does not, by itself, falsify the historical landings.

## Finding on the broad staged-landing alternative

### `NOT CURRENTLY SUPPORTED ENOUGH TO DISPLACE THE CREWED-LANDING MODEL`

A broad staged model remains admissible and reopenable.

However, to displace the current finding it must explain the **joint evidence field**, not only survive isolated anomalies. Depending on the specific staged variant, it would need to account for some combination of:

- lunar-distance tracking and communications;
- equipment at multiple lunar sites;
- returned lunar material and differentiated geology;
- surface experiments and ranging targets;
- later orbital/site consistency;
- contemporaneous mission documentation and engineering chronology;
- independent non-US observations.

Merely showing that one image is misleading, one explanation is incomplete, or one anomaly is unresolved does not by itself establish a staged model.

Equally, the Court will not permit the conventional account to answer a material image-level contradiction merely by citing institutional authority.

## Weighted conclusion

> **The Court presently finds that the Apollo crewed lunar landings are supported by a STRONG weight of convergent evidence (`W4`). The finding is not absolute and is not closed to challenge. Material anomalies remain admitted, particularly in image-level morphology, exhibit provenance and the long replication gap. Those anomalies reduce the finding from a maximal weight and require further adjudication, but on the present record they do not outweigh the independent and convergent evidence supporting crewed lunar operations. The broad staged-landing alternative remains possible in the logical sense but is not presently supported by an evidentiary model strong enough to displace the crewed-landing finding.**

## What would materially change this finding?

The Court would reopen and potentially reduce or reverse the weight if new evidence showed, for example:

1. a verified physical or chronological impossibility in a core mission operation;
2. a reproduced image-level contradiction that cannot be reconciled with the claimed equipment geometry or sequence;
3. collapse of a major independent evidence stream through provenance failure or fabrication;
4. a staged alternative that explains the joint evidence field with fewer unsupported assumptions than the crewed model;
5. raw independent orbital imagery materially contradicting reported site configuration;
6. sample-provenance failure substantial enough to undermine the returned-material corpus;
7. or a broader architectural failure in OGI's weighting process itself.

Until such a trigger is shown, the current weighted finding governs.
