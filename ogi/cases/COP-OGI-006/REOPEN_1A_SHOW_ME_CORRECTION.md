# COP-OGI-006 — Reopen 1A: SHOW ME correction

**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Change class:** `CORRECTION / EVIDENCE-RUNG CLARIFICATION`

The researcher challenged the statement that the Chandrayaan-2 Apollo 11 image had been `LOCATED / PUBLISHED` when the prior response had only produced a peer-reviewed paper describing the image.

The challenge is sustained.

> **A paper saying that an image exists is not the same evidentiary rung as showing the image.**

## What is now actually shown

A displayed image attributed to ISRO has now been retrieved. It shows the Apollo 11 landing-site region with an annotation identifying `Eagle Descent Stage`.

Stable displayed-image file:
- https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ea/Chandrayaan-2_Apollo.jpg

Provenance page:
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chandrayaan-2_Apollo.jpg

That provenance page records:
- description: image of the Lunar Module Eagle descent stage from the Chandrayaan-2 orbiter;
- source: 3 September 2021 livestream `GUJCOST Celebrates Vigyan Utsav`;
- author: Indian Space Research Organisation;
- displayed file dimensions: 755 × 572 pixels.

The underlying 2024 *Planetary and Space Science* paper separately states that OHRC imaged the Apollo 11 site on 2 April 2021 at ~0.26 m spatial resolution and that the LM is visible in the OHRC image.

## What is still not shown

The Court has **not yet retrieved and inspected**:
- the original raw/calibrated PRADAN OHRC product used for the 2 April 2021 observation;
- its exact product identifier;
- the corresponding XML/PDS geometry metadata;
- a direct pixel chain from the archive product to the displayed/annotated image;
- a Court-side independent rendering of the raw file.

Therefore the current staircase is:

`PAPER_DESCRIBING_OHRC_IMAGE = RETRIEVED / INSPECTED`

`DISPLAY_IMAGE_ATTRIBUTED_TO_ISRO = RETRIEVED / SHOWN`

`DISPLAY_IMAGE_SOURCE_CHAIN_TO_2021_ISRO_PRESENTATION = LOCATED`

`RAW_PRADAN_OHRC_PRODUCT = NOT_YET_RETRIEVED`

`RAW_PIXEL_TO_DISPLAYED_IMAGE_DERIVATION = NOT_YET_REPRODUCED`

The prior shorthand `INDEPENDENT_SUB_METRE_APOLLO_11_SITE_IMAGE = LOCATED / PUBLISHED` is therefore too compressed and is superseded by the rung-by-rung state above.

## OGI lesson

This correction is itself a useful test result:

> **Do not let a source asserting the existence of an exhibit impersonate the exhibit.**

A citation may locate a claim about an image. It does not automatically retrieve, inspect or reproduce the image.
