# COP-OGI-003 — Court Weight-of-Evidence Finding

**Finding ID:** `COP-OGI-003-WEIGHT-001`  
**Status:** `CURRENT WARRANTED FINDING / REOPENABLE`  
**Legal standing:** `NONE`

## Central propositions and weights

### The fatal Titan decision is adequately explained as a foolish passenger ignoring an obvious danger
`UNSUPPORTED — W1 WEAK`

The waiver disclosed catastrophic risk, so passenger agency remains real, but that does not reconstruct the actual engineering-information field available to the passenger.

### The passenger made a voluntary decision inside a materially defective safety-information and oversight field
`SUPPORTED — W5 VERY STRONG`

Official investigations, operator records, the absence of conventional certification, ignored or marginalized engineering concerns, prior-success normalization and organizational power dynamics strongly support a defective decision field.

### Prior successful dives, founder confidence, expert participation and expedition professionalism constituted an independent engineering warrant
`UNSUPPORTED — W1 WEAK`

These were trust proxies, not substitutes for independent validation of hull life and safety margins.

### The private psychology of any specific deceased passenger is reconstructed by this case
`NOT ESTABLISHED — W1 WEAK`

Without direct contemporaneous evidence from that passenger, the Court may reconstruct the field but not invent the person's internal reasoning.

## Weighted conclusion

> **The Court finds very strong weight (`W5`) for a distributed decision failure: individual consent occurred inside a materially defective engineering, oversight and information field. The evidence does not support reducing the event to passenger foolishness, nor does it erase passenger agency. The strongest current explanation is therefore personal choice operating within a distorted risk field, not either actor-only blame model.**

## Reopen triggers

- new direct communications materially changing what a particular passenger knew;
- new authoritative engineering findings altering the technical causal chain;
- evidence materially changing the availability or meaning of the trust proxies identified.

**Court posture:** `SHOW ME.`
