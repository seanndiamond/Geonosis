## Search limitations / research debt

- `NOT_SEARCHED`: complete private communications of every fatal passenger.
- `INACCESSIBLE`: any non-public pre-dive due-diligence material held privately by passengers/families.
- `NOT_ESTABLISHED`: exactly which public warnings each passenger personally read before committing funds.
- `NOT_ESTABLISHED`: how much each passenger relied on founder confidence, prior dives, expert presence, price, status or expedition framing.
- `SEARCH_METHOD_LIMITED`: this first run did not exhaust every testimony transcript or every USCG exhibit.

## Evidentiary rule
The case may reconstruct the field but may not claim to reconstruct a deceased individual's private mental state without direct evidence.
