This case tests whether OGI can handle an original intuition that rejects an observation as impossible without merely flipping into credulous acceptance when later recurrence appears.

The originating research event came from Sean Diamond's early Saqsayhuamán reading. A rabbit-like humanoid sovereign figure appeared in the working image-first interpretation before the later functional name `Ra` was attached. The initial intuition was that animal people, as a concept, could not exist.

The case distinguishes:

1. the existence of animal-human composite representations;
2. the recurrence of a rabbit-man motif/function within the Geonosis working corpus;
3. the biological possibility of interspecies chimerism in modern science;
4. the much stronger claim that a literal tall rabbit-human people existed historically.

Current result: the absolute prior `IMPOSSIBLE` is not warranted, but the opposite literal-ontology claim is also not established.

> **The intuition was wrong to close the question. The evidence has not yet earned the opposite conclusion.**
