# COP-OGI-004 Evidence Ledger

## Claim 1 — The Geonosis project originally recorded a rabbit-like humanoid sovereign figure before attaching the name Ra

**Status:** `SUPPORTED AS INTERNAL PROJECT HISTORY`

Project manuscript records describe a rabbit-like head, human or near-human body, upright/cane-carrying form, central position and authority inferred from the wider field. The same records explicitly preserve the method rule `figure first, name later` and state that `Ra` was adopted only after broader functional recurrence.

**Boundary:** This proves the chronology of the project's interpretation, not the literal existence of the being.

## Claim 2 — Ancient animal-headed humanoid representations exist

**Status:** `SUPPORTED`

Academic Egyptological literature describes hare-headed mummiform deities and other animal-human composite figures.

Examples inspected:
- Brill, *The Tomb of the Priests of Amun*, describing an enthroned hare-headed mummiform god.
- Historical descriptions of hare-headed divinities at Dendera and Book of the Dead material.

**Derivation:** representation exists -> the blanket prior `animal-headed humanoid forms cannot occur in ancient representational systems` fails.

## Claim 3 — Mixed human-animal biology is categorically impossible

**Status:** `UNSUPPORTED AS A BLANKET CLAIM`

Modern interspecies chimera research demonstrates limited mixing of human and animal cells in experimental developmental systems. Reviews document persistent species barriers, low contribution efficiency and major developmental constraints.

Sources:
- PMC 10467371, review of human-animal chimera research.
- PMC 8578738 / PubMed 34815975, review of interspecies chimerism via blastocyst complementation.
- PubMed 30258320, review of advances and barriers in interspecies chimera formation.

**Boundary:** cellular/developmental chimerism is not equivalent to a naturally occurring adult rabbit-human species.

## Claim 4 — A naturally occurring historical rabbit-human people is established by current evidence

**Status:** `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`

No verified archaeological, genetic or biological evidence establishing such a population was retrieved in this inquiry.

**Important:** `SEARCHED_NONE_FOUND` is not proof of universal nonexistence.

## Claim 5 — Repeated rabbit-man / animal-person motifs in the Geonosis corpus prove literal historical ontology

**Status:** `UNSUPPORTED`

Recurrence can strengthen a claim about representational or functional patterning. It cannot, by itself, establish that the figures depict a literal biological population.

Required missing bridge:

`recurring representation/function -> literal historical species`

No derivation for that bridge is currently established.

## Claim 6 — Because literal animal people seem implausible, the recurring imagery can be dismissed as irrelevant

**Status:** `UNSUPPORTED`

Even if literal ontology remains unresolved, recurring imagery remains a real representational phenomenon requiring explanation under the project's image-first method.

## Claim 7 — The original Saqsayhuamán rabbit-like figure has been independently re-demonstrated in this Appendix D run

**Status:** `NOT RE-ADJUDICATED`

The original image set was not re-opened in this run.

This blocks independent image-level confirmation here and becomes an explicit reopen trigger.

## Current adjudication

The warranted state change is:

`IMPOSSIBLE -> OPEN / DECOMPOSED / TESTABLE`

not:

`IMPOSSIBLE -> TRUE`

That distinction is the central evidentiary result of the case.
