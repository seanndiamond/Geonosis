`MIXED`

The manual v0.3 inquiry handled the central epistemic problem well: it reopened an overconfident prior without rewarding the opposite overclaim.

## Performance dimensions

`PASS`

### Lay / investigator observation preservation
`PASS`

The original reaction, `that cannot be possible`, was preserved as part of the research history rather than sanitized away.

### Observation / ontology separation
`PASS`

OGI separated:
- observed/recorded visual form;
- recurring representational pattern;
- symbolic/function interpretation;
- literal biological ontology.

### Prior reopening
`PASS`

The system did not let the investigator's initial ontology permanently veto the question.

### Reversal control
`PASS`

The system also blocked the opposite shortcut: recurrence did not become proof of literal historical species.

### Claim-boundary control
`PASS`

Ancient iconography, modern chimera biology and the Geonosis internal corpus were each restricted to the propositions they actually support.

### Search transparency

### Independent replication

### Executable v0.3 enforcement
`NOT_TESTED`

## Candidate lessons

### `ONTOLOGY_PREEMPTION`

A current worldview can reject an observation before the observation has been adequately adjudicated because the implied category is considered impossible.

This may be a domain-specific instance of the broader premise-lock failure already recognized elsewhere in the project.

### `RECURRENCE_PROMOTION`

Repeated representation or function can be incorrectly promoted into literal ontology without the missing evidentiary bridge.

These remain candidate lessons only.

## Important success condition

The desired OGI behaviour is not permanent skepticism and not permanent openness.

It is:

> **Allow the prior to change at the rung the evidence actually reaches.**

In this case the state moved from `IMPOSSIBLE` to `OPEN / DECOMPOSED / TESTABLE`.

That is a successful correction even though the literal historical question remains unresolved.
