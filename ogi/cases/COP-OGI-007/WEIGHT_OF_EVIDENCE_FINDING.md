# COP-OGI-007 — Court Weight-of-Evidence Finding

**Finding ID:** `COP-OGI-007-WEIGHT-001`  
**Status:** `CURRENT WARRANTED FINDING / REOPENABLE`  
**Legal standing:** `NONE`

## Central propositions and weights

### Policy-relevant science benefits from independent replication, audit and quality assurance beyond ordinary peer review
`SUPPORTED — W4 STRONG`

Peer review is not experimental reproduction. The reef-fish replication controversy, later misconduct findings concerning specific researchers, and broader reproducibility concerns materially support Ridd's methodological demand for stronger QA.

### James Cook University's initial censure of Ridd's expert scientific criticism was justified
`UNSUPPORTED — W1 WEAK`

The High Court of Australia held the 2016 censure unjustified under the intellectual-freedom provisions applicable to the dispute.

### JCU's eventual termination of Ridd was legally unsustainable merely because the original censure was wrong
`UNSUPPORTED — W1 WEAK`

The High Court upheld the ultimate dismissal because later findings included conduct outside the protected core of the original scientific criticism, particularly confidentiality/process breaches.

### Reef institutions never scientifically engaged Ridd's challenge
`UNSUPPORTED — W1 WEAK`

Published scientific rebuttals and replies exist. Scientific engagement occurred even though that engagement is not equivalent to independent replication of every disputed proposition.

### Ridd's methodological challenge is therefore proven to validate all of his substantive Great Barrier Reef conclusions
`UNSUPPORTED — W1 WEAK`

Legal and methodological success do not transfer automatically into scientific truth. Reef propositions remain claim-by-claim.

### The institutional field materially entangled scientific correction, reputation and disciplinary authority
`SUPPORTED — W4 STRONG`

The original criticism concerned the reliability of work associated with institutions whose reputations were implicated, and the response included both scientific rebuttal and disciplinary action later found in part to have improperly constrained protected criticism.

## Weighted conclusion

> **The Court finds strong weight (`W4`) for Peter Ridd's central methodological claim that policy-relevant science requires visible, independent quality assurance beyond peer review and institutional reputation. It finds very strong legal support for the proposition that JCU's initial censure improperly constrained protected expert criticism, while also recognizing that the eventual dismissal was legally sustainable on later non-protected conduct. The Court does not find that these outcomes validate all of Ridd's substantive reef claims. The strongest surviving finding is therefore methodological and institutional: a legitimate correction challenge entered a field in which scientific rebuttal, institutional reputation and disciplinary authority became materially entangled.**

## Reopen triggers

- new legal material changing the interpretation of the High Court outcome;
- independent replication materially resolving disputed reef propositions;
- stronger evidence concerning institutional QA procedures, incentives or disciplinary causation;
- demonstrated errors in the source corpus used for this finding.
