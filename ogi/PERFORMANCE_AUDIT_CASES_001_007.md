# OGI v0.3 — Cross-Case Performance Audit, COP-OGI-001 through 007

**Audit ID:** `OGI-PERF-001-007`  
**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** `CURRENT WARRANTED PERFORMANCE FINDING / REOPENABLE`  
**Legal standing:** `NONE`

## Purpose

Assess OGI/Oggy against seven deliberately different inquiry fields after freezing a weighted Court finding for each case.

The audit separates:

1. value of the **human + Oggy + Court workflow**;
2. Oggy's ability to preserve the method without human correction;
3. validation of the still-partly-specified v0.3 architecture.

## What Oggy demonstrably did well

### 1. Claim decomposition
Across cases, Oggy repeatedly separated propositions that ordinary discussion tends to collapse: exposure from poisoning, representation from ontology, waiver from actual risk state, authority from derivation, equipment-on-Moon from human emplacement, legal vindication from scientific truth.

**Finding:** `W4 STRONG`

### 2. Safe unresolved states
The system repeatedly retained `UNKNOWN`, `NOT RETRIEVED`, `NOT RE-ADJUDICATED`, and `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE` rather than forcing binary closure.

**Finding:** `W4 STRONG`

### 3. Ability to credit authority when authority earns it
Armstrong and Ridd demonstrate that OGI is not structurally anti-institutional. USADA gained weight through its exposed evidentiary chain; the High Court gained weight on the legal propositions it actually adjudicated.

**Finding:** `W4 STRONG`

### 4. Correction and reopen behaviour
Apollo is the strongest demonstration. Multiple researcher objections materially changed the derivation, and the system preserved the failed earlier state rather than silently rewriting history.

**Finding:** `W4 STRONG`

### 5. Field-before-actor analysis
Glyphosate, Titan and Ridd show useful reconstruction of objectives, proxies, incentives, trust, authority and correction pathways instead of default actor blame.

**Finding:** `W4 STRONG`

## Where Oggy failed or remains weak

### 1. The method is not yet self-enforcing
The most serious limitation is unchanged from Case 001. Oggy often followed v0.3 well **after the researcher reminded it of the governing method**. Apollo showed that it could still inherit a conventional explanation before adjudicating the antecedent proposition.

**Finding:** `W2 LIMITED` for autonomous method retention.

### 2. Exhibit status was sometimes promoted too quickly
In Apollo, a paper saying an Indian orbital image existed was initially treated as though the exhibit itself had been shown. That violated the Court staircase.

Candidate failure: `EXHIBIT_ASSERTION_SUBSTITUTION`.

### 3. Plausible explanation sometimes arrived before exhibit match
Footpad geometry, rover tracks and LM exterior explanations were initially given more weight than the supplied images warranted.

Candidate failure: `EXPLANATION_BEFORE_EXHIBIT_MATCH`.

### 4. Observation status was downgraded by language
Direct image observations were at one point called "suspicions". That changed the epistemic posture before adjudication.

Candidate failure: `OBSERVATION_DOWNGRADE`.

### 5. Evidence independence needs machine enforcement
The Apollo case exposed the danger of treating multiple NASA-derived artifacts as independent confirmation. Armstrong also shows that institutional adoption of one evidentiary record is not automatically a new independent evidence stream.

Candidate failure: `INDEPENDENCE_COMPRESSION`.

### 6. Some analogies can be evidentially distracting
In the rabbit-man case, modern interspecies chimera biology is sufficient to defeat an overly broad claim of categorical biological impossibility, but it does not materially support a naturally occurring historical rabbit-human population. The system kept the boundary, but such analogies require explicit relevance control.

**Finding:** `W3 SUBSTANTIAL` boundary control, with improvement needed.

## Candidate lessons now supported across more than one case

### `DERIVATION_EARNS_AUTHORITY`
Supported by Armstrong and Ridd. Authority should rise only at the propositions for which the institution exposes a strong derivation.

**Promotion recommendation:** `PROMOTE TO FORMAL PRINCIPLE`.

### `INDEPENDENCE_COMPRESSION`
Material in Armstrong and Apollo. Multiple institutions or artifacts may depend on the same underlying evidence chain and must not be counted as separate confirmations without dependency mapping.

**Promotion recommendation:** `PROMOTE TO FORMAL FAILURE MODE`.

### `TRUST_PROXY_STACKING`
Explicit in Titan and structurally present in institutional science: prestige, prior success, expert presence, peer review and professional presentation can collectively impersonate a missing primary warrant.

**Promotion recommendation:** `PROMOTE WITH NARROW DEFINITION`.

### `CORRECTION_REJECTION`
Already present in OGI and strongly reinforced by Ridd: a field can suppress or punish a materially relevant correction because it conflicts with local institutional incentives or proxies.

**Status:** `REINFORCED`.

## Cross-case Court finding on OGI

### Human + Oggy + Court workflow as a research process
`SUPPORTED — W4 STRONG`

The seven cases show substantial ability to handle contradictory evidence, preserve uncertainty, distinguish claims, credit and challenge institutions symmetrically, reopen findings, and convert researcher observations into testable propositions.

### Oggy as an independently self-governing implementation of v0.3
`LIMITED — W2`

Apollo proves that the model still requires active human challenge to maintain the method reliably. Several critical v0.3 controllers remain specification-level rather than executable.

### OGI v0.3 architecture as validated engineering
`SUBSTANTIAL BUT PROVISIONAL — W3`

The cases provide real behavioural evidence that the architecture is useful, but they do not yet demonstrate that the architecture is implemented strongly enough to govern fresh models or agents without active researcher correction.

## Overall conclusion

> **Oggy has performed well enough to justify continuing the architecture, but not well enough to declare the architecture solved. The strongest result is the research partnership itself: under active Court discipline, the human-AI system produced strong corrections, explicit unknowns, differentiated claims and recoverable derivation chains across radically different domains. The principal failure is equally clear: too much of that discipline still lives in the human researcher and the written specification rather than in executable controls.**

The next engineering question is therefore not whether OGI can produce intelligent analyses. It can. The question is whether the controls that made the best analyses possible can be made **persistent, automatic and testable when the researcher does not catch the drift first**.

**Court posture:** `SHOW ME.`
