# Geonosis

**Reading the field before naming the form.**

Geonosis is an independent research programme developed by Sean Diamond for testing whether visual, spatial, archaeological, biological, and terrain-based systems can preserve structured information through relation, recurrence, placement, boundary, sequence, and form.

The project uses **Méthodê Glyph** as its formal visual method and **Picterpretation** as the act of treating images as primary epistemic artefacts rather than merely as illustrations of inherited textual explanations.

The governing discipline is simple:

> **Observe first. Map relations. Test constraints. Then interpret.**

## The audit standard

Geonosis does not treat a catalogue label, translation, historical attribution, or conventional classification as self-proving. Where a claim depends on a visible artefact or field, the project asks for the derivation chain.

A strong claim should be able to show, where relevant:

- the original artefact, site, image, or source field;
- the exact visible mark, feature, relation, or location being discussed;
- the proposed identification;
- plausible competing identifications or values;
- the reason for selecting one interpretation over another;
- the operational, grammatical, spatial, or construction step that follows;
- the failure conditions that would make the reading weaken or collapse;
- enough source and viewing information for another observer to repeat the test.

A citation demonstrates that a claim has been made. It does not, by itself, demonstrate that the claim has been derived from the visible field.

## Claim ladder

The repository separates four levels of claim:

| Tier | Claim type | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Published/observable background | Cite and identify the source field accurately. |
| 2 | Operator interpretation | Demonstrate repeated behaviour in constrained context. |
| 3 | Grammar hypothesis | Show recurrence, distribution, sequence, controls, and alternatives. |
| 4 | Speculative extension | Label explicitly and never use it as proof of the lower tiers. |

This separation is deliberate. Imagination is allowed into the laboratory, but it has to wear a name tag.

## Core principles

- **Field before form.** Do not let an inherited label erase what is visibly present.
- **A caption is not a translation.** Classification begins an audit; it does not finish one.
- **Method before conclusion.** Case studies do not prove the method merely by being interesting.
- **Return and recurrence matter.** A useful operator should repeat under constraint, not appear once in a favourable crop.
- **Failure is evidence.** A hypothesis that cannot lose is not a useful research instrument.
- **AI is an audit partner, not an oracle.** AI-assisted analysis should expose source chains, alternatives, uncertainty, and failure conditions rather than manufacture confidence.

## Publication boundary

This is a **public research repository**, not the complete private working archive. Material that still contains unresolved image rights, heritage-sensitive coordinates, incomplete provenance, unverified source labels, or unfinished citation work is represented here by a status record rather than silently promoted to publication-grade evidence.

That boundary is part of the method.
