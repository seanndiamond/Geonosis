# Sean Diamond

Sean Diamond is a Melbourne-based author, sculptor and independent researcher. After two decades in corporate management, he turned to sculpture and later developed [Geonosis](README.md), an image-first research programme for investigating ancient objects and landscapes. His sculpture includes *The Crucible*, the fire-breathing steel dragon installed at Melbourne’s White Night. [Read the interview in The Westsider.](https://thewestsider.com.au/a-conversation-with-sean-diamond/)

## Books

### Twelve Lambs: The Girl in the Clay

This novel grew out of Sean’s image-first study of a three-centimetre clay tablet that The Metropolitan Museum of Art catalogues as a receipt of lambs. As he rotated, reflected and overlaid photographs of the tablet, recurring shapes suggested a girl he named Elataya. The novel imagines her life and the world she grows up in. Its opening chapter includes some of the images that prompted the story. [View the tablet at The Met (11.217.19).](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/322463)

### Intelligence Is Not a Ladder

Drawing on life with his German Shepherd, Donny Brook, Sean asks what humans, animals and AI each bring to a shared task, when machines should stop, and who carries responsibility for the decisions that follow. [View the book listing.](https://www.thriftbooks.com/w/intelligence-is-not-a-ladder-ai-human-judgement-and-the-cow_sean-diamond/60898927/)

## Research

Geonosis begins with the object or image, records how each reading was produced, and gives other readers a way to examine and challenge it.
