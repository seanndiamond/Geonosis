## Two distinct records, not a manufactured agreement

**The Met institutional reading:** “receipt of lambs”. This is a catalogue classification. To inspect its derivation, recover the published transcription, line inventory, numerical units, associated formulae and original incisions linked to this exact accession.

**Sean Diamond's cold mirrored-overlay reading:** C1001–C1006 is preserved unchanged, including C1002A's return and the experience → education → formation → maturity → graduation → social work interpretation. In this reading the claimant did not identify a definitive lamb form corresponding to the collection title.

**Finding:** neither “the label is a demonstrated phonetic translation” nor “the cold visual reading proves the museum title incorrect” is established by this addendum. The object is now precisely identified, making same-artifact comparisons possible. No asserted numeric-ration/barley reading is to be imputed to this object.
