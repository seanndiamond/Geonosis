## What was actually done

The claimant reports selecting the grey tablet on the same day, rapidly glancing through it without knowing its provenance, published translation or subject, then conducting an initial ('cold') visual reading. The operation was **mirrored overlay**, **not** merely a normal overlay: a reflected copy of the tablet image was positioned edge-to-edge and moved gradually (a swipe/slide), with apparent relational forms changing with translation and viewing scale. The claimant described beginning at a corner that appeared to express the purpose; different corners may perform different functions. The reader zoomed, pulled back, tracked features through successive alignments, and revisited earlier positions when new forms appeared.
