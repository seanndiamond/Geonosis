## C1007 — From the operator into the multi-being field

Claimant starts at the central column: a stern experienced operator with incoming information at the ears and multiple units/components to manage. Above the operator are sequential animal-like forms: cat, bull, smaller rabbit-like being with black dot and large ears, elephant-like form with large ears and nose, and finally an open mouth interpreted as an operator/commander within whose realm the graduate works. The source field on either side is busy with beings of differing sizes, including proposed giants and smaller animal-morphology people. Right side includes a dog-related bite image that can read as biting a hand or a baby's lower body at different scales; closer view may suggest an eagle in a dog's mouth. The claimant reads this as the need to exercise restraint and discrimination. Dog head and tail suggest an animal communicating with the operator. Lower register includes baby and young children; a possible threatening being restrained by another figure with a tool; a smiling woman engaging with a bird; multiple differently sized beings engaged in social activity. **Reading: the trained operator enters a complex and heterogeneous civilisational field and must coordinate varied relationships and risks.**

## C1008 — Operator in society: restrained force, animal coordination and care

**Claimant's original account for this configuration (faithful structured transcription; no retroactive changes to meaning):**

1. **Base / ontology:** At bottom a dark patch is the responsive-stone foundation of the tabernacle ontology; it connects back to the inverted tabernacle form read on the graduate's garment in Part One.
2. **Gathering / protection:** Immediately above, the trained operator's arms surround **four smaller beings** who are cooperating and communicating. She coordinates and protects them and wears a cap-like protective form.
3. **Open palms and field regulation:** Above, her torso appears with **both arms extended, palms openly facing the viewer** like a traffic controller. She is directing activity rather than simply attacking. Behind her the claimant sees possible buildings or factories of civilisation.
4. **Other morphologies:** A monkey-like face appears in the centre of her chest; this is read as including monkey-type beings or capacities within the varied activity she must coordinate.
5. **Dog and bite discernment:** Her right arm leads to a small dog head. Below is the bite form: a mouth enclosing an eagle head, but through variation of viewing scale the form can resemble a baby's body. The claimant's critical interpretation is **training and commanding the degree of bite force appropriately**, including avoiding harm to vulnerable children while retaining the capacity to restrain or seize a target.
6. **Vulnerable beings:** Below is a baby and another prone/reclining figure; sleeping, incapacitated or deceased status unresolved.
7. **Right-hand social environment:** Crowded zone contains police-like figure, woman smiling and playing with a bird, barking small dog as distraction, bird, two rabbit people, a larger person, and giant beings above. The operator interacts with many social roles, species/morphologies, sizes and distractions, and must respond differently to each.
8. **Overall interpretation:** The educated graduate is an active, competent participant in civilian life, coordinating others and protecting vulnerable beings while exercising the responsible control of animal force. Capabilities are applied within varied relationships under the underlying tabernacle/responsive-stone ontology.

**Author phrase:** “she's dealing with it all elements of society at some stage of her interactions. And she has to know how to do each one differently”.

## C1009 — “Undercover”: protection of the tabernacle and controlled communications

**Image source supplied by claimant:** `c1009 under cover.jpg`. Image is a user-supplied mirrored-overlay still; its binary is **not deposited** by this text commit.

**Reading status:** AUTHOR'S ONGOING LIVE INTERPRETATION, recorded verbatim in originating conversation and structured here without claiming independently verified ancient meaning.

**Central figure / assigned role.** The claimant identifies the previously established graduate/operator at the middle, maintaining her police-like posture and aptitude. A tall, vertically open form in the forehead/third-eye position is read as concentrated attention restricted to a specific field or job.

**Working tabernacle and protection.** Immediately below the head, a V-shaped form is read as the tabernacle; below the point of the V, another outline suggests the tabernacle operating on responsive material, with what the claimant calls a green stone at its centre. Additional lines before the V represent a protective boundary or defended working zone. The operator is now interpreted as protecting both the tabernacle ontology and potentially the physical apparatus.

**Two inward-looking sentinels.** On each side of the working tabernacle, the claimant sees two female faces turned inward, wearing dark glasses. Their common orientation is read as deliberate watching and safeguarding of the central operation.

**Upper security operator.** At the top is a larger head with sunglasses. The claimant names it a “man in black”, associating it with secret / high-clearance / black-budget operations. This is the claimant's functional analogy, not evidence of a literally modern secret service.

**Lower vessel and directed personnel.** At the bottom the operator has a more structured protective cap. Her arms are now understood not as embracing a group, but as *holding a boat-like vessel seen from the front*, with two beings on board (left and right). The claimant reads her as commanding or directing the boat's operators.

**Bird communication array.** Multiple bird-like shapes appear at the bend of the arms and along the arms' upper edges. The claimant reads these as communication devices/capabilities linking the operative to multiple parts of the civilisation. He explicitly contrasts a single bird with a distributed network, estimating approximately “three, five, ten birds”. **Do not silently replace this with a precise count.**

**Summary of claimant's interpretation.** C1009 presents an authorised operator with disciplined, narrow-field consciousness and broad communications access, supervising a tabernacle-related operation, protecting its material and information, coordinating sentinels and field agents, and working within a restricted or confidential civilisational role.

**Author phrasing:** “I'm going to call the panel undercover”; “she's in the field. She's protecting the tabernacle”; “she's got some pretty serious clearance”.
