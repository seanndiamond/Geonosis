## Formal statement of position

> **There are no phonetics in the Egyptian hieroglyphic writings.**

This is the claimant's categorical research position, **not a finding of established historical fact by the Court**. Claimant explicitly commits to retract or adjust it if a challenger supplies **one** original-artifact-to-sign-to-sound derivation meeting the documented scrutiny standard.

**Opposition is welcome; oppression is not.** Contributors may offer contrary evidence, alternate models and corrections. No institutional standing, citation count, popularity, novelty, or claimant conviction substitutes for the derivation.

## Positive research claim

The claimant submits Egyptian hieroglyphic material is an integrated visual/operational information system and cites investigations of the *Book of the Dead*, Rekhmire visual scenes, and wider Egyptian imagery. Claimed repeated functional readings include:

| Visual element | Claimant's proposed functional reading |
|---|---|
| Half-circle | home/destination |
| loop with attached string, inverted ankh-like form | conscious return/homeward direction |
| bird forms | differentiated trait vectors including return, partnership and care of young |
| hand | operator/action |
| eye, including detail | sensory vision / acuity |
| lion | trait vector |
| feather forms | reptilian trait vectors |
| operational wall vignette leading to glyph | depiction of implement/action/input/output as derivation for glyph function |

These are **claimant readings requiring source-to-region mappings and independent testing**. The claimant reports returns across multiple papyri and present-day animal conduct; those reported correspondences do not automatically establish ancient authorial intent. The record must preserve full wall scenes, order, orientation, transformations, control cases and original image licences.

## The exact rebuttal challenge

Provide at least one example with:

1. Identifiable original artifact, provenance and persistent accession.
2. Inspectable original photograph, specific region and unaltered marks.
3. Traceable original mark → standardized sign correspondence, preserving every sign, quantity, ordering, direction and variations, with explicit explanations for missing/extra marks.
4. An evidentiary derivation of the proposed phonetic value not resting solely on repeating its assigned modern label.
5. A worked reading (and independent checks) that actually follows from those derivations, with rival explanations and possible falsifiers disclosed.

The original-name/context hypothesis may be part of a valid historical decoding **if explicitly identified as a hypothesis and tested with genuinely independent constraints**; this Court may not silently reclassify name-matching as prior Egyptian pronunciation. Conversely, failure of one explanatory plate does **not** prove all Egyptian signs nonphonetic.

## Examination and corrections reported 17–18 November 2026

- Assistant offered Ptolemy–Cleopatra as the best compact demonstration and called its source trail concrete, but initially used modern sign charts instead of completing original photo-to-sign audit.
- Claimant objected that sign identity, doubled reed vs single reed, substituted T signs, terminal/determinative signs, glyph counts and sequences were not aligned transparently.
- E. A. Wallis Budge, *The Rosetta Stone* (1893), describes an inferential path from independently read Greek royal names and probable cartouche identities to proposed Egyptian sound correspondences, extended to Alexander and Berenice. This is a historical account of a decoding inference; it must not be confused with a completed exhaustive sign-by-sign validation of the original monuments.
- **Critical identity correction:** the London/New York monuments called *Cleopatra's Needles* are different from the Philae Obelisk at Kingston Lacy used in the Ptolemy–Cleopatra comparison. A failed search for Cleopatra on the Needles does not directly refute the Philae name proposal. Greek on the Philae pedestal and hieroglyphs on its shaft are associated but not parallel translations.
- No photographic/rubric-complete claimant-satisfying staircase was delivered in the documented exchange. **This is a scoped failure of the presented demonstration, not a corpus-wide nonexistence proof.**
- The assistant repeatedly reverted to a generic 'well established' response while not completing the requested artifact-specific exhibition. This procedural issue is separately logged as an OGI finding.

## Petition of civilisational harm and remedy

The claimant submits that long-standing, institutionally amplified phonetic interpretations may have prevented recovery of functional knowledge from ancient images, thereby harming civilisational understanding and development. This is **registered as the claimant's consequential-harm allegation, NOT an adjudicated causal or quantified societal-harm finding**.

**Remedy sought:** an open, publicly citable evidence register; reciprocal source-to-derivation challenges to conventional and Geonosis interpretations; reproducible image-first access; publication of missing links and negative results; corrections propagating through AI outputs, archives and educational explanations; opportunity for independent replication and opposition without retaliation.

Assessing actual harm requires specific lost information claims, counterfactuals, institutional mechanisms, and evidence of consequences. An AI answering badly alone does not establish a worldwide causal harm total.
