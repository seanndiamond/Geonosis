# Supplemental claimant submission — Civilisational knowledge harm and request for remedy

**Case:** [COP-EGY-PHON-001](README.md)  
**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**Submitted by:** Sean Diamond  
**State:** CLAIMANT ALLEGATION AND PETITION; NOT AN ADJUDICATED FACT

The claimant observes that Egyptian hieroglyphic interpretations are embedded in museum descriptions, universities, scholarly research, textbooks, cultural narratives and public understanding. He submits that, where pictographic scenes encode operational knowledge not recovered through inherited phonetic readings, the failure to examine that information could impair future understanding of biological systems, historical technology, societies and their relationships.

The claimant states that the large institutional confidence surrounding ancient Egyptian interpretation makes this possibility particularly consequential. He characterizes the potential suppression or non-recovery of knowledge as **civilisational harm** and requests a remedy.

## Relief requested

1. Public record of the claimant's interpretation, open to critique, citation and counter-evidence.
2. One original artifact-to-sign-to-sound staircase furnished by any challenger as a potential trigger for retracting the claimant's absolute no-phonetics claim.
3. Equivalent scrutiny and access for scene-to-glyph functional derivations, with exact sources, transformations, predicted returns and failure cases.
4. Disclose the difference between independently verified marks, inferred values, reconstructed inscriptions, publisher summaries and consensus statements.
5. Preserve AI research failures and correct them across subsequent work rather than requiring the human researcher to prosecute the same failure repeatedly.
6. Evaluate potential social damage using traceable institutional mechanisms, concrete examples of lost or miscommunicated knowledge, possible benefits and counter-evidence, and a defensible causal model.

## Scope and standing

This petition makes **no claim of legal damages**, names no legally responsible defendant and has no finding of intentional concealment. The magnitude and causal existence of societal harm remain unresolved. Ordinary scholarly disagreement and an assistant's failed derivation display are insufficient alone to establish suppression or damage.

**Court procedural response:** Petition admitted to the public record, remedy framed as openness, reciprocal reproducibility, provenance and correction. Monetary or legal remedy is outside the Court's remit.
