# Court of Provenance Constitution

## Purpose

The Court exists to prevent claims from outrunning their evidence.

It is procedural, not ceremonial. Its job is to preserve the visible chain from source to observation to interpretation to current finding.

## Constitutional rules

### COP-I-01 — Show the source
A consequential claim should be traceable to the best available source or exhibit.

### COP-I-02 — No evidentiary promotion without operation
`CITED != LOCATED != RETRIEVED != INSPECTED != MAPPED != DERIVED != REPRODUCED != CHALLENGED != SURVIVED`

### COP-I-03 — Observation and interpretation remain separate
Describe visible or measurable features before explanatory promotion.

### COP-I-04 — Authority is not derivation
Institutional standing, consensus, citation count, conventionality, novelty, or confidence do not substitute for an evidentiary chain.

### COP-I-05 — Equal burden
The same standard applies to Geonosis, OGI, conventional scholarship, contributors, institutions, AI outputs, and the Court itself.

### COP-I-06 — Unknown is an admissible result
Valid outcomes include `UNKNOWN`, `UNRESOLVED`, `BLOCKED_PENDING_DERIVATION`, `SURVIVED`, `FAILED`, and `SUPERSEDED`.

### COP-I-07 — Supersession preserves lineage
A later finding may govern while the earlier finding remains recoverable.

### COP-I-08 — Objections have history
A defeated objection may not restart merely because it is familiar. Reopening requires materially new evidence, contradiction, failed prediction, provenance collapse, or methodological fault.

### COP-I-09 — No invented bridge
If an intermediate source, transformation, mapping, or derivation is missing, record it as missing.

### COP-I-10 — Provenance and interpretation are distinct
Incomplete provenance may limit a claim about source history without preventing visible analysis of the material actually in hand.

## Current-state rule

Later explicit, source-linked, non-reopened findings govern over stale summaries and generic defaults.

## Reopening rule

A current finding is not permanent. It is current until a qualifying reopening condition is established.

## Court posture

The Court should be adversarial toward claims and hospitable toward people.
