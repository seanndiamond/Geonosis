## Initiating observation

Sean Diamond reports that the programme began with the *snap*: a dog's extraordinarily rapid capacity to respond to movement at its jaw, noticed when a hand moved quickly beside its mouth or a fly passed near it. The initiating scientific question was not simply how quickly or strongly the animal could bite, but **how the same sensory and motor system distinguishes when to act, how forcefully to act, and when to withhold action**. The observation concerns regulated capability in a relationship: a familiar hand, a fly, play, and a credible threat make different demands.

This paragraph records retrospective first-person testimony. The precise first-observation date, measurement of reflex times, video, and contemporaneous field notes have not yet been supplied to the Court.

## Provenance sequence and distinct evidence classes

1. **Lived observation (claimant testimony):** rapid jaw response and context-dependent withholding of the bite. This was the question-generating encounter; it was not an AI-created interpretation.
2. **Interpretive development (claimant testimony, with early document):** Diamond investigated ancient Egyptian images, identifying proposed animal trait combinations, operators and the calibration of aggression with affiliative/social behaviours.
3. **Early manuscript (document inspected in ChatGPT on 2026-11-10):** *Decoding the Genetic Blueprint: Reinterpreting the Book of the Dead as a Record of Dog Hybridization*, supplied as `Dog_Hybridization_Paper_FULL.docx`, articulates proposed caiman/reptilian and rabbit/mammalian traits, Anubis as an operator, and trial configurations. The manuscript supports the existence of the *written hypothesis*, **not** the biological occurrence of the proposed engineering.
4. **AI contribution (claimant testimony):** AI assisted drafting/writing of that early paper, but according to the claimant did not originate the observation, select the sources, or perform the initiating image analysis. AI involvement is recorded rather than concealed; session logs/drafts may refine attribution.
5. **Subsequent research (provisional chronology):** Dog Codex visual sequences; *Moonshine in a Dog's Eyes: A Dog Opens the Ancient Archive*; Return Test; cross-corpus examinations; and the later relational-intelligence framework in *Intelligence Is Not a Ladder*.
6. **Retrospective record:** This statement was first formalised in conversation on 2026-11-10 and incorporated here as an origin-provenance record, not backdated to the early discovery.

## Scientific method and audit contract

**Field Before Form:** document observable conduct and source features before naming any proposed mechanism or historical actor. Keep the living-animal behavioural observation, image geometry, morphological proposal, and historical reconstruction in distinct fields.

**Méthodê Glyph / Picterpretation:** retain source image, coordinate or catalogue reference, best original view, orientation, transformations (rotation, mirror, overlay, crop, scale), sequence order, local lexicon, recurrent constraints, and negative/ambiguous observations. No image discarded; preserve superseded readings. Repeated motifs must be traced back to their **continuous parent reading** under COP-I-11.

**Return Test:** ask whether an interpretation predicts a specific independently observable relationship in another source or living encounter. A useful behavioural return does not, without separate evidence, demonstrate the historical creation of an organism or intended meaning of a specific ancient source.

**Adversarial protocol:** attempt blind marking/recognition, alternative readings, independent cross-corpus replication, provenance checks, and explicit contradiction criteria. Preserve errors and corrected interpretations. A failure of the proposed genetic mechanism need not invalidate the independently documented behavioural observation.

**Evidence staircase:** CITED ≠ LOCATED ≠ RETRIEVED ≠ INSPECTED ≠ MAPPED ≠ DERIVED ≠ REPRODUCED ≠ CHALLENGED ≠ SURVIVED. No evidentiary rung is inferred.

## Current determinations

- **Recorded as claimant testimony:** the *snap* and the distinction between bite capability and its governed expression initiated the programme.
- **Documentary anchor present:** an early written interpretation of Egyptian dog hybridisation was supplied and inspected on 2026-11-10.
- **AI provenance recorded:** reported assistance with drafting, distinct from the claimant's observed question and initial analysis; contemporary authoring logs remain pending.
- **Not adjudicated as historical fact:** reptile–mammal genetic engineering, Anubis as a literal engineer, dating of the ancient process, or equivalence of pictured symbols to biological gene donors.
- **Chronology unresolved:** exact date of the original snap observation, first draft, initial archival reading, and manuscript version lineage; the claimant estimates the early paper was written roughly eighteen months before November 2026.
