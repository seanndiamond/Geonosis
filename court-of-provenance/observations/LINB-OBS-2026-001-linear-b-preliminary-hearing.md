# LINB-OBS-2026-001 — Linear B Preliminary Hearing

**Status:** OPEN

**Trigger:** challenge to the proposition that Linear B phonetic values possess a complete derivational staircase from artefact to sound.

## Conventional case examined

The Court traced the conventional decipherment through Kober and Ventris.

Kober's structural work established repeated sign relationships before sound values were assigned.

The first phonetic values were subsequently introduced through hypotheses that selected Knossos sign-groups represented known Cretan place-names. Those candidate sound values were propagated through the structural grid and later produced Greek-looking vocabulary and morphology.

### Interim conventional finding

**STRUCTURAL RECURRENCE:** ADMITTED.

**FOUNDATIONAL PHONETIC DERIVATION:** NOT PROVEN TO COURT STANDARD.

The Court located no independently anchored ancient acoustic value forcing the accepted phonetic values from primary evidence without relying on hypothesised lexical identification, place-name matching, later Greek comparison, or the already-constructed phonetic system.

The KN Ra weapon / "pa-ka-na" example does not provide an independent acoustic anchor because the conventional object identification and the phonetic reading participate in the same interpretive network.
