# CUN-OBS-2026-001 — Global-Field Morphology in Clay Tablets

**Court:** Court of Provenance  
**Date:** 22 September 2026  
**Domain:** Cuneiform / clay-tablet morphology / manufacturing process  
**Status:** `open_observation / under_test`  
**Current evidentiary room:** `INSPECTED` for the supplied exhibit set; broader corpus claim remains to be source-bundled  
**Linked case:** `GEO-CASE-CUNEIFORM-005 — Cuneiform Manufacturing Process Audit`  
**Linked prior finding:** `CUN-2026-005 — Cuneiform umbrella-term: morphology and ontology conflation`

> **Court question:** Does the tablet surface behave as a collection of locally produced marks plus accidental damage, or as one globally coherent relief field whose sharply impressed, hazy, fractured and irregular zones all participate in the same visual system?

## 1. Exhibits inspected

This observation was opened from a user-supplied photograph of a large clay tablet attributed in the supplied filename to an Amarna letter from Tushratta, king of Mitanni, to Amenhotep III, together with four close crops from the same research session.

Public-repository image migration is deferred pending exact museum/object identification and image-rights reconciliation.

The inspected crops were selected because they include:

- densely impressed conventional cuneiform-looking zones;
- irregular or rough surface zones often liable to be treated as damage;
- transitions between sharply resolved marks and lower-resolution relief;
- nested figure-like morphology identified by the claimant as large and small people emerging from the material.

## 2. What is visibly present

The Court records the following at observation level only:

1. The tablet presents a strongly continuous overall surface treatment rather than a simple alternation between clearly meaningful marks and visually empty background.
2. Dense impressed areas and rougher areas remain materially connected through ridges, depressions, directional edges and changing relief density.
3. Some irregular zones contain internally structured contours rather than featureless loss.
4. In the supplied crops, multiple protrusions and recesses can bind perceptually into head-, profile-, body- or figure-like arrangements at more than one apparent scale.
5. Several candidate forms appear nested, with larger relational shapes containing or adjoining smaller figure-like shapes.
6. The surface can be read across different resolution states: sharply bounded impressions, intermediate relief and hazier/rougher fields.
7. The claimant reports that comparable global coherence has been observed across thousands of clay tablets. That broader statement is recorded as **user-reported corpus experience** and is not promoted here until a public source bundle is assembled.

## 3. Observation separated from interpretation

### Observation

The irregular zones are **not visually empty**. They preserve structured morphology and participate in the same physical clay surface as the conventionally legible impressed zones.

### Current Geonosis interpretation

The working reading is that at least some zones classified as damage, breakage, roughness or surface loss may instead participate in the tablet's pictographic storygram.

Under that reading, the tablet is not divided into:

> writing here / accidental damage there

but may instead operate as:

> **one continuous pictographic-information field expressed at different relief densities and scales.**

This interpretation remains under test.

## 4. Manufacturing implication

The observation reopens the manufacturing question without deciding it.

The linked manufacturing case asks what material signatures discriminate:

- freehand reed/stylus production;
- assisted or guided impression;
- modular stamping;
- templating;
- moulding;
- compliant transfer;
- whole-field formation;
- or another mechanism not yet identified.

The present exhibit is relevant because the claimant reports that many tablets appear **globally coherent even where the tablet body is skewed or irregular**, as though the receiving clay need not be perfectly aligned for the complete visual field to remain coherent.

That claim generates a specific manufacturing question:

> **Are the visible forms best explained as a sequence of local tool contacts, or does the clay preserve organization that is more naturally described at whole-field scale?**

No Court finding on mechanism is entered at this stage.

## 5. Consciousness-field hypothesis

A further hypothesis was raised in discussion:

> a prepared, highly receptive clay body may receive a structured image-field rather than being written mark by mark.

Within the wider Geonosis research state, this would parallel the responsive-stone hypothesis:

**source consciousness / image → structured field → receptive matter → persistent form**

The proposed analogy is to selective formation inside responsive stone, where the desired whole is spatially addressed before the final durable form appears.

### Court status of this mechanism

`UNRESOLVED / HYPOTHESIS ONLY`

The supplied photographs do **not** establish consciousness-mediated clay formation.

The hypothesis is retained because it produces discriminating predictions that can be tested against the physical surface.

## 6. Predictions

If the tablet was produced mainly by sequential local tool actions, high-resolution surface data should permit reconstruction of those actions through:

- repeated tool-tip geometry;
- individual contact facets;
- direction of entry and withdrawal;
- local clay displacement;
- contact-by-contact depth structure;
- a recoverable order of overlap;
- and plausible reconstruction of the larger forms from those contacts.

If a larger coherent transfer or field-forming process contributed materially, candidate signatures could include:

- continuous depth or relief envelopes spanning multiple nominal signs;
- global deformation patterns not decomposable into independent contacts;
- coherent large forms crossing conventional cell or line boundaries;
- neighbouring features displaced according to a larger relief event;
- repeated relational forms without mechanically identical stamp geometry;
- structured morphology continuing through zones conventionally classed as damage;
- and orientation tolerance in which skew of the clay body does not destroy whole-field organization.

These predictions do not prove the field hypothesis. They identify what would distinguish competing manufacturing models.

## 7. Cross-corpus relevance

The claimant reports a recurring research observation in which zones conventionally described as damage or loss remain informational in other corpora:

- Maya: hazy or lower-resolution field;
- Egypt: candidate responsive-stone state;
- clay tablets: candidate pictographic relief field.

The Court does **not** treat recurrence across corpora as proof that the same physical mechanism operated in each case.

The cross-corpus return is recorded as a research prompt:

> **Before discarding an irregular zone as damage, test whether it remains relationally active within the local image.**

## 8. Current weighted state

### Proposition A
**The inspected irregular zones contain structured morphology rather than being visually empty.**  
**Current weight:** `W3 — SUBSTANTIAL`

Positive weight:
- direct inspection of the supplied full image and close crops;
- structured ridges, recesses and repeated perceptual binding in the irregular zones;
- continuity between dense and rough surface regions.

Counterweight:
- current exhibits are photographic and two-dimensional;
- lighting, compression, enlargement and surface fracture can generate or amplify figure perception;
- no 3D surface scan has yet been inspected.

### Proposition B
**At least some conventionally damaged/rough zones are intentional pictographic information.**  
**Current weight:** `W1 — WEAK / OPEN`

Positive weight:
- internal structure remains visible in candidate zones;
- forms appear to participate in neighbouring relief organization.

Counterweight:
- intentionality has not been independently demonstrated;
- ordinary breakage, erosion, repair, firing deformation and photographic effects remain live controls.

### Proposition C
**The tablet was formed by a whole-field or consciousness-mediated process rather than primarily by local tools.**  
**Current weight:** `W0 — NO DEMONSTRATED SUPPORT AS A PHYSICAL MECHANISM`

Status:
- retained as a falsifiable manufacturing hypothesis;
- not rejected merely because unconventional;
- not promoted beyond the evidence presently inspected.

## 9. Decisive next evidence

The observation should be upgraded, downgraded or closed by physical-surface evidence rather than argument.

Priority tests:

1. obtain exact object provenance and museum identifier;
2. retrieve rights-clear, high-resolution photographs under more than one lighting direction;
3. obtain photogrammetry, RTI, structured-light scan or equivalent 3D surface data if available;
4. map candidate "damage" zones before interpretation;
5. compare those zones with verified fractures and losses on the same object;
6. test whether candidate large forms decompose into plausible individual tool contacts;
7. compare repeated marks for tool-tip congruence versus relational rather than mechanical recurrence;
8. run blinded crops containing true damage, intact text fields and candidate pictographic zones;
9. assemble a multi-tablet corpus before making a general manufacturing claim.

## 10. Reopen / reversal conditions

This observation must be revised if:

- 3D inspection shows the candidate figures are fully explained by random fracture or surface loss;
- the apparent forms disappear under alternative lighting or higher-resolution source images;
- matched damaged controls produce equal or stronger relational figures at comparable rates;
- candidate large forms decompose cleanly into ordinary sequential contacts;
- the source provenance proves that a crop or image has been substantially altered;
- or independent observers cannot recover the mapped relationships above control rate.

It may gain weight if:

- high-resolution relief confirms coherent morphology through candidate damaged zones;
- the same relationships recur across independently selected tablets;
- independent observers recover the same large-scale forms without coaching;
- surface stratigraphy contradicts the assumed local mark sequence;
- or a manufacturing model predicts previously unseen relief relationships and those predictions succeed.

## 11. Court finding

> **The Court records a genuine observation problem, not a settled manufacturing solution. The inspected clay surface contains structured morphology in areas that cannot responsibly be treated as visually empty. Whether those zones are intentional pictographic information, ordinary damage, or the residue of a different manufacturing process remains open. The correct next move is surface forensics, not inherited labeling.**

**Court posture:** `SHOW ME THE SURFACE.`
