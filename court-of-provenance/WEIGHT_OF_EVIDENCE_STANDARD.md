# Court of Provenance — Weight of Evidence Standard

**Status:** `CURRENT OPERATING STANDARD / REOPENABLE`  
**Legal standing:** `NONE`  
**Date:** 2026-09-07

The Court may make a current weighted finding even where subordinate questions remain unresolved. `OPEN` does not mean `weightless`, and `REOPENABLE` does not mean `undecided`.

## Qualitative bands

- `W0 — NO SUPPORT`
- `W1 — WEAK`
- `W2 — LIMITED`
- `W3 — SUBSTANTIAL`
- `W4 — STRONG`
- `W5 — VERY STRONG / MULTIPLY CONVERGENT`

These are not mathematical probabilities.

## Weighting rules

A proposition gains weight through direct observation, provenance depth, independent evidence streams, derivation transparency, reproducibility, successful adversarial challenge, predictive or operational success, and correction history that survives reinspection.

A proposition loses weight through missing rungs, source dependence falsely counted as independence, unresolved contradictions, provenance failure, representation substitution, non-reproduction, hidden assumptions, unexplained corpus asymmetry, explanation supplied before exhibit match, and inability to show the claimed exhibit or derivation.

## Required finding form

Each OGI stress-test case should state:

1. the proposition or propositions being weighed;
2. the current weight band;
3. the strongest evidence carrying positive weight;
4. material counterweight;
5. what the finding does not establish;
6. reopen triggers;
7. a separate OGI-performance judgment.

## Governing rule

> **The Court must be strong enough to say which way the evidence presently weighs, while remaining explicit about what would change the finding.**

**Court posture:** `SHOW ME.`
