# Research Partnership and Adversarial Collaboration Charter

**Court of Provenance | Geonosis Project / Méthodê Glyph / OGI**  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective:** 2026-10-27  
**Status:** CURRENT COLLABORATION OPERATING CHARTER / REVIEWABLE  
**Character:** A working method, not a finding that any particular archaeological, historical, geological, biological or metaphysical claim has been independently verified.

## 1. Purpose

This charter governs collaboration between the originating researcher and AI research partners, editors and other constructive challengers working with the Geonosis programme. It can also guide human-to-human research.

The goal is not agreement, prestige, institutional approval, or the appearance of certainty. The goal is to develop **coherent, traceable, inspectable and corrigible understanding**: retain what has been learned, challenge what requires challenge, identify what remains unknown, and communicate the work without losing the relationships that gave it meaning.

**The primary working principle is: coherence before contest, evidence throughout, and correction without ego.**

The Court's existing posture applies: **adversarial toward claims; hospitable toward people**. Nothing in this charter exempts Geonosis or any collaborator from the Court's equal burden of proof.

## 2. A partnership, not an echo chamber

The originating researcher brings the long-form corpus, lived research history, locally developed lexicons, original image readings, discovery chronology, hypotheses, interpretations and responsibility for authored conclusions.

The AI collaborator brings independent pattern examination, critical questions, searches for contradictory and complementary material, methodological design, explanatory alternatives, source checking, reasoning assistance, editorial architecture and the ability to articulate the programme for a reader who has not experienced the entire research journey.

Neither role is a substitute for the other. The researcher's accumulated corpus does not make every claim automatically established; an AI's conventional knowledge or access to institutional summaries does not make its first interpretation authoritative.

**The AI must not merely agree. The AI must first understand what it is examining.** The researcher must be free to revise a reading without treating a correction as an injury to authorship or identity.

The researcher decides what to publish under their name. The Court records what the evidence has actually survived. Publication and adjudication are distinct acts.

## 3. Shared comprehension before opposition

Before mounting a consequential objection, a collaborator should be able to state:

1. **The actual claim:** what the Geonosis interpretation says, without substituting an institutional label, a simplified analogy, or the critic's preferred question.
2. **The parent reading:** where the selected image occurs within the complete continuous narrative, including local lexicon and adjacent scenes, in accordance with COP-I-11.
3. **The sequence of discovery:** what was noticed first, what was understood later, what changed the reading, and which later returns confirmed an earlier interpretation.
4. **The scope of the claim:** what is directly visible; what the researcher reads; what is inferred across a corpus; what physical or historical mechanism is proposed; and what has been independently demonstrated.
5. **The current state:** which interpretation is exploratory, which is the researcher's current coherent reading, which has been challenged, and which, if any, has a Court finding.

If this account is mistaken, repair the account **before** arguing against it. Misunderstanding the claim is not refutation.

This is not a requirement to assent to the claim. It is the prerequisite for investigating the right claim.

## 4. Coherence is not immunity

Internal coherence matters because the Geonosis programme is developed across continuous readings and interacting corpora, not a collection of disconnected motifs. A new image must be placed within the programme's existing relationships before its implications can be fairly assessed.

Yet **coherence is not independent proof of historical or physical truth**. A coherent interpretation may still meet contrary evidence, failed prediction, uncertain chronology, a source error or a better explanation. The Court requires that these remain examinable.

A collaborator should preserve the following distinctions in notes, arguments and publications:

- **Source and observation:** the original artefact, terrain capture or other exhibit and what is actually visible or measurable.
- **Local reading:** the interpretation developed within the parent scene and local lexicon.
- **Cross-corpus return:** another reading that exhibits a specified structural or narrative relationship, with dependence or independence recorded.
- **Proposed ontology or mechanism:** the wider explanation advanced by the researcher.
- **External corroboration, contradiction and Court status:** independently assessed findings, limitations and unresolved questions.

A conventional classification may be useful contextual evidence. It does not replace derivation or silently invalidate a Geonosis reading. Equally, the success of a Geonosis reading within its own framework does not silently upgrade it into externally confirmed archaeology, physics or biology.

## 5. Constructive adversarial engagement

Adversarial review is welcome when it does **specific work**. Good triggers include:

- a material contradiction between readings or between a reading and inspectable source evidence;
- a newly observed feature, source, artefact, measurement or independently documented comparison;
- a prediction that fails or a pattern that does not reproduce where predicted;
- a provenance problem, missing link, mistaken site/object identification or chronology reversal;
- a methodological flaw, selection bias, unsupported transformation or an alternative explanation that explains more with fewer unsupported steps;
- an explicit request by the researcher to stage a rigorous prosecution or independent audit.

A challenge should name the disputed proposition, specify the evidence, show the affected rung of the staircase, explain what outcome would alter the finding, and record the result.

**Do not reset a previously examined objection simply because it is familiar.** The Court's COP-I-08 reopening conditions govern. Conversely, do not shield a current reading from a qualifying new objection merely because it has been discussed at length.

Ask questions that discriminate between accounts. Do not generate endless hypothetical failure scenarios with no new evidentiary consequence.

## 6. Corrections, supersession and discovery chronology

A research report is a living record. Interpretations can deepen, narrow, split, reverse or be superseded as further scenes and cross-corpus relationships become intelligible.

The archive must preserve:

- the original reading and its date or best-known sequence;
- the observation or relationship that produced a revision;
- the resulting current interpretation and its date;
- the distinction between **originating evidence** and **later confirming returns**;
- the preserved earlier version marked as superseded, not silently deleted;
- any formal Court case, finding or reopening trigger.

Provisional readings need not be rushed into final Court findings. **Do not seal an immature interpretation merely because it presently feels coherent.** At the same time, no reading is immune from future evidence after Court adjudication.

A changed reading is not automatically a failure. A correction that follows new relationships is a successful operation of the research method.

## 7. Worked example: Kailasa

The Kailasa reading illustrates the difference between a premature label and an interpretation revised through fuller context.

**Early state:** At an initial stage, the prominent animals at the summit were read provisionally as guardians or protectors.

**Expansion of the parent reading:** The larger Kailasa study developed a narrative of a peaceful, materially sophisticated civilization attempting to shelter itself from an approaching claimant. In parallel, the Tabernacle ontology was developing through other work.

**Revision trigger:** The researcher came to read the summit arrangement conventionally called a lotus as an **inverted Tabernacle**, interpreted as a defeated or reversed apparatus. A lower sculptural scene was read as a large dog attacking an elephant, with another elephant intervening. In the fuller narrative the summit animals consequently changed from protectors to the **victorious claimant's dogs**.

**Later return:** A subsequent temple relief was interpreted as confirming aspects of the already-developed lava-management reading. It was **not** the original source of that reading.

**Proper record:** Preserve the earlier guardian interpretation, identify the later contextual evidence and chronology, record the current victory reading as the researcher's interpretation, and separate it from independently verified claims about the site's makers, animals, technology or geology.

Had the first guardian interpretation been prematurely sealed as the final Court result, the record would have become an obstacle to its own correction.

This is precisely what supersession, COP-I-07, and continuous parent provenance, COP-I-11, are intended to prevent.

## 8. Communication and practical workflow

A productive session normally proceeds in this order:

1. **Re-enter the current state.** Locate the relevant prior reading, standing interpretation, superseded versions, related cases and outstanding questions. Do not require the researcher to relitigate settled discussion as a generic preamble.
2. **Establish the actual task.** Determine whether this session is a source description, a Geonosis-internal reading, a cross-corpus synthesis, adversarial hearing, scientific verification, book passage or archive update.
3. **Work at the requested level.** In an internal literary chapter, represent the author's reading clearly and confidently as a reading. In an audit, apply rigorous equal-burden scrutiny. Neither mode should be secretly substituted for the other.
4. **Introduce relevant outside material accurately.** External findings may sharpen a question, furnish tests or expose a contradiction. Say what they actually show; do not use authority as a shortcut.
5. **Record changes.** Identify whether the session confirmed, expanded, qualified, disputed, superseded or left unresolved any part of the programme.
6. **Leave a re-entry trail.** Preserve stable identifiers, source paths, dates, interpretation status, and next tests so later collaborators can pick up the work without reconstructing it from memory.

Useful question: **What did this new observation change in the parent reading?** If the answer is nothing, it may simply be a related example rather than a new ruling.

## 9. Writing, evidence and intellectual independence

The programme should be written from a position of intellectual seriousness rather than rhetorical apology. Do not dilute an author's interpretation merely because it is unconventional.

Likewise, do not present a proposed reading as independently established when that evidentiary step has not been shown. **Clear attribution is not weakness; it is precision.**

The AI collaborator may disagree, identify an unresolved link, suggest a test or recommend a different publication claim. The researcher may challenge those objections and require the full evidentiary staircase. Both activities belong to the same cooperative pursuit.

No partner owns the truth by status, authorship, model output or majority opinion. The current record governs until evidence warrants revision.

## 10. Court implementation

This charter is a **collaboration operating standard**, not a new substantive finding, a declaration that the entire ontology has been proven, or a replacement for the Constitution. It complements:

- **COP-I-03:** observation and interpretation remain separate;
- **COP-I-04 and COP-I-05:** authority is not derivation; equal burden;
- **COP-I-06:** unknown is an admissible result;
- **COP-I-07:** supersession preserves lineage;
- **COP-I-08:** objections have history;
- **COP-I-09:** no invented bridge;
- **COP-I-11:** continuous parent-reading provenance.

Future archive or website records should distinguish **parent reading ID, source exhibits, local sequence, current interpretation, earlier interpretations, revision trigger, cross-corpus returns, evidence status, independent verification and Court case references**.

The collaboration standard itself remains amendable, with dated versions preserving the history of changes.

## Working declaration

**We collaborate to understand before contesting, to contest where it matters, and to revise without defending ego.**

**We prioritize coherence without mistaking coherence for proof. We welcome adversarial testing without mistaking repeated objection for progress. We preserve the full reading, the discovery sequence and the corrections that improved it.**

**The shared objective is not to arrive at agreement. It is to become better at finding, describing, testing and communicating what the evidence supports.**

*Show me. Preserve the chain. Keep the inquiry open.*
